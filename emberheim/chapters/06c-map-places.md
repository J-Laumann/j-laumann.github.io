#### Heaven's Reach

The mesa and mountain country on the west of the map, with a blue oasis lake. Highrest and Castle Darkstone are printed here.

#### The Sea of Sand

The dune sea below the Reach. Dusthaven stands in it. Scorpion Gulch is printed in the same desert, and the bounty sheet calls it a three-day ride south of Dusthaven. In 973 Carter Wint found a Node of Air somewhere in this desert. The sheet does not mark the spot.

#### The Ember Plains

The green middle of the map. Ironspire sits where the plains, the river, and the forest meet. The present railway runs from Ironspire to Steamwood, about 80 miles, and from Dusthaven to Steamwood, about 125 miles. North of Ironspire, past wooded hills and a small sea from the Enigma, is Mundane. Soldiers of the Empire also have a private train from Ironspire to Fort Ironclad, about 50 miles, and from Steamwood to Fort Ironclad, about 65 miles.

#### The Savagebloom Expanse

The far southeastern jungle. Anchorhall is in the north of it. Venomshade stands in its darkest reaches. Bloomridge is the Flameguard camp inside it. Talia Moss, the Verdant Viper, comes from the Expanse. The Lost City of Gold is in this jungle, and it has not been found.

A treacherous, ever-expanding jungle, where nature reigns and stays untamed. Colossal trees with serpentine roots blot out the sun. Their leaves shimmer with an otherworldly glow. Under the canopy the jungle is full of vividly colored venomous creatures and ancient sentient plants with a hunger for flesh. The air is thick with magic, and the growth swallows whatever stands in it. Explorers speak of flora that bloom with poisonous beauty and vines that coil like serpents. Deeper in, the place pulses with a primal, malevolent energy.

At the end of the Orcish Uprising, in 935, orcish refugees fled toward this jungle. The wildlife, the goblins, and the yuan-ti slaughtered them. Gorc, under Goblin Camp, is the child who was left.

##### Goblin Camp

**GM.** The bird-cries drop away. Low murmurs, harsh laughter, and a whiff of acrid smoke come through the trees. The trees part on a ragged clearing, barricaded with sharpened stakes and knotted vines. Makeshift teepees of wood and hide. Goblins, green and gray, move between them. Masked goblins dance at a roaring fire and roast something down to char. A couple more play a tribal tune on crude instruments.

The largest hut sits at the high point, backed by a short cliff and the river. Bone totems decorate the entrance. In front of it a deep pit has a ramp down. Inside the pit are desiccated bodies and one living prisoner, chained to the stone. He is a Flameguard soldier from Bloomridge. He and an ally were attacked and captured. The ally was tortured to death. He promises a reward if he is returned to Bloomridge. He is not named. In Lost City the hook was not used. From Brews to Bullion reuses this camp, and he is there again to be rescued.

Gorc is an orc man the camp took in as a child, having mistaken him for a goblin while he wandered the jungle. He does not know his heritage. He comes from those refugees of 935.

#### Bloomridge

A small camp set in the Savagebloom Expanse for the ground it holds, built around a cluster of ancient temples. The soldiers are the Flameguard. They use fire magic to keep a clearing open against the jungle. The temple stone is blackened with soot. The flicker of flame is the camp's sign that it is still standing, and the jungle's shadow is never far.

The rooms are General Ignar, Aeris Thorne, The Burning Barrel, a general store, the stables, and the church. The general store has no scene of its own. It sells the ordinary list in [Mundane goods](#mundane-goods). The camp names no shopkeeper, stablehand, or priest of its own. Dusthaven's open bounty Axe Beaks is a request from this camp: dead axe beaks, 10 gp each, for artisans assembling and exporting axes.

**GM.** Rex Refund is known here as Floyd Hibiscus Sr. He is the open Dusthaven bounty, and he is one of the two who failed to find the Lost City of Gold. Scorpion Jack was with him. Nothing says he is in the camp.

##### The Burning Barrel

Food: Blazing Skewers, spicy grilled jungle-boar with a chili glaze and herbs, 2 sp. Ember-Roasted Vegetables, charred jungle vegetables with exotic peppers, wild mushrooms, and a smoky dipping sauce, 3 sp. Inferno Steak, steak of a jungle beast with fire spices, sizzling jungle-root potatoes, and grilled onions, 8 sp. Scorched Serpent, grilled jungle snake in a spicy citrus glaze, with rice, 5 sp. Blaze Beans, spicy black beans with jungle peppers and herbs, and a sprinkle of wild greens, 2 sp.

Drink: Anything Ale, a robust ale of leftover fruit with a smoky undertone, 5 cp. Flame Kissed Cider, spiced cider with fresh fruit, 1 sp.

**GM.** The Burning Barrel is a tent and the camp tavern. Garrick "Firebeard" Stonefist owns it and runs it, an old dwarf with a singed silver beard.

##### General Ignar

**GM.** General Ignar Voryn. Over six feet, muscular, weathered and bronzed by the jungle and by battle. His eyes are an intense amber, said to mirror the flames he commands. His voice is calm and steady, gravel under it. He wears enchanted flame-resistant armor and is rarely without Blazebane, a massive flame-forged greatsword that crackles with inner fire when drawn. He is a strategist and a ferocious fighter, loyal to his soldiers, and set on holding Bloomridge against the Expanse.

He has a casual relationship with the camp's head scribe, Aeris Thorne.

##### Aeris Thorne

**GM.** Miss Aeris Thorne, a young-looking half-elf. Magical glasses. Long black hair under a tall crumpled wizard hat. Her tent holds a single cot, a couple of tables covered in scattered books and papers, and old objects and pieces of stone from lost temples. The Goblin Map says information on the local ruins can be gleaned from some of Bloomridge's scribes. She is that scribe.

#### Venomshade

In the darkest reaches of the Savagebloom Expanse. An ancient temple, now a yuan-ti lair. It was a place of forgotten worship. A poisonous mist shrouds it. Venomous vines and corrupted flora have grown over the stone. The yuan-ti have made it a den of serpentine power, where they plot and conduct dark rituals. The air smells of venom and decay, and the ground seems to pulse. Few who go near come back. Those who do speak of scales on stone and unblinking eyes in the dark.

#### The Lost City of Gold

The city has no official name yet. Legend calls it The Lost City of Gold. An Aetherian name is still to be chosen. In 971 Rex Refund and Scorpion Jack searched for it and did not find it. The Goblin Map and the River of Ssstyx are the trail. Nothing on those pages says the two reached any of the rooms below. In 980 Thaddeus Moorwind gives that same Goblin Map to the From Brews to Bullion party. The jungle sites are in play again.

Decades ago, in the Era of Chaos, an extremely powerful wizard rose over a pocket of order in this jungle. A bustling city of stone had grown there, a hub of commerce and peace, holding against the restless growth. It had amenities nobody hoped to find in such a place. Some say that in the morning sun the city looked made of gold, its gilded temples catching the light. The wizard rued that growth. He thought only power should rule these lands, and that this was not a place for man to conquer nature. It was, to him, the last bastion of natural order. He caused an apocalypse. Powerful magic erupted from the heart of the city. On an ordinary day the people were suddenly gone: every living being instantaneously petrified, soul trapped in a terracotta statue. He took the city and hid the crime inside a powerful barrier that conceals it as more jungle. It lies lost, with a single immortal man in his solitude and the power of his army of terracotta souls. He has grown more powerful with every year. What lies deep in the city now is not written. The Era of Chaos is not a line on the Historical Timeline.

##### River of Ssstyx

**GM.** The river fans out into a small lake. Stone ruins dot the lake, peeking from the murky water. The ruins that stood here were overtaken by the jungle and swallowed. Across the lake a pair of stone slabs stand out of the mud, like doors, with a green gem in the middle that vaguely resembles a snake's eye.

Fire held up to the gem makes it glisten, and the doors slide open onto a stone staircase down into the dark. The steps end at a hallway heavy with moisture. Murals of overlapping snakes seem to shift whenever the eye leaves them. A dripping sounds far ahead.

Three pressure plates are in the hallway, DC 15 to find. Stepping on one shoots two darts from the snake murals, +5 to hit, 1d4 piercing and 2d10 poison, halved on a DC 14 Constitution save.

The dripping leads to a large subterranean room. Water drips through the earth and off dozens of stalactites into a lake, then trickles over the far side, where it can be heard rushing below. A single mossy stone bridge lies in ruins. Stones on each side reach toward each other and do not meet. The gap is about 15 feet. Water weirds live in the lake and attack only when something disturbs the water.

Past the gap, steep slick stairs descend beside a waterfall fed by the lake. The waterfall on the worn stone drowns out other sound. A river forms and runs ahead, with a cavern path beside it. Bleached bones come up out of the frothy rapids, and skeleton yuan-ti climb out to attack.

Farther on, the rapids slow and the river widens. It loops, then turns sharp left and falls into dark depths with no visible end. A small stone bridge still spans the place where the river seems to loop on itself. Over that short bridge is a stone altar. Snake carvings decorate the sides. On the smooth top, treasure: about 100 gold coins strewn with a few gems and trinkets, and on the pile a large golden cube.

The cube is about the size of a fist. Each of the six faces has delicate geometric patterns, interlocking lines, and angular symbols, of elven craftsmanship. The patterns seem to shift when the angle changes. The sheet describes the cube twice, in the same words. No obvious lock. Some of the carvings feel as if they could be pressed, twisted, or turned. A faint magical hum. It is a puzzle. No solution is written.

Touching the treasure throws the top of the altar aside. An undead yuan-ti chieftain rises. The water here deals necrotic damage. The sheet gives no amount.

#### The Hive

The dark country east of the river. A Dusthaven bounty, Hive Sighting, sends hunters toward a small hive near Scorpion Gulch. Venomshade is not this country. It stands in the Savagebloom Expanse. The Hive itself has no other scene.

#### The Midlands

South of the Hive. A small unnamed settlement.

#### Fort Ironclad


**GM.** A dome of pure steel, sinking into the marsh. An imperial military outpost, built to hold back the Hive and watch the Enigma. The metal is already rusting in the acidic air. Burnt gunpowder can be smelled almost a mile out. When it has to, the lid of the dome splits and a colossal cannon pushes out. With enough condensed mana, gunpowder, and a purpose-built round, the weapon can be fatal to anything within miles.

A private train runs here from Steamwood, about 65 miles, a day at 6 hours 30 minutes, 5 gp 2 sp, and from Ironspire, about 50 miles, a day at 5 hours, 4 gp. Only soldiers of the Empire are allocated a seat.

#### Frosthook

The horn of ice in the northern sea. Permafrost Prison and Glacier's Edge are there. In 980 a Node of Air was spotted in the Frosthook mountains, and the Flying Boats company set out for it.

#### Permafrost Prison

On the Frosthook ice. Oswald "Friendly Face" Price escaped from it.

#### Glacier's Edge

On the Frosthook ice. The railway joins it to Mundane, about 125 miles. That ride is a day, 12 hours 30 minutes, and the fare is 10 gp. Mundane itself is the town north of Ironspire.

#### Tim

In the clouds north of Frosthook.

#### Azure Lagoon


**GM.** A breathtaking archipelago in the northeastern ocean. Crystalline blue water, lush islands, coral reefs, hidden coves, bone-white sand. The central lagoon glows an almost magical azure, especially under the moon. Mundane and mystical wildlife. Salt and tropical flowers. Ancient ruins of a forgotten civilization, secrets under the waves. Beauty, and something unfinished.

In the summer of 980 an Auran agent came through a node near the lagoon and was killed. He named Yan-C-Bin.

##### The Isle of Falling Stars

An isolated island in the Azure Lagoon, rumored to exist only during certain phases of the moon. Sailors tell of rainbow colors glittering on the night horizon when they sail southeast. Digs & Dives lists it.
