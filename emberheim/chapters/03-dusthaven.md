#### Dusthaven

**GM.** Elowen Sunweaver founded Dusthaven in 906. She is an elven archmage of fire and sun, now a lich, living deep in the Enigma. The town does not know she founded it, does not know where she is, and does not know she is a lich. Those three facts have always been kept secret. Her tower is now the mayor's office, and her sealed journals are still in it. See People and Aetheria.

What Dusthaven tells is the town sheet's story: decades ago a human explorer found an oasis of condensed mana in the far west of Emberheim, in an unforgiving desert, and the town exploded out of it. The place is rough, full of beasts and bandits, and famous for bounty hunting. The Sheriff's Office posts outlaws, rogue magitech inventors, and the irregular creatures that threaten the town.

The Weird West company now knows more than the town does, and less than the truth. They know Elowen created the temple that housed the Sun Prism, and that she is a powerful wizard. They do not know she founded the town, where she is, or that she is a lich. The current mayor is Thorian Brightshield.

Named locations: Sheriff's Office, Gunther's Guns, Spare-a-drop Supplies, the Lounging Lizard Inn, the railway station, the stables, and the church.

The Marauder war, in order, is in Weird West. The cave they used while Zephyr and Sirocco led is in Miscellaneous locations.

##### Sheriff's Office

**GM.** Double doors, clinking metal, the hum of arcane machinery, a whiff of gunpowder. A stern deputy in a duster and a ten-gallon hat shuffles wanted posters at the central desk. Behind him an ornate clock shows floating, glowing roman numerals, and a self-playing piano fills the room. Posters of outlaws and fugitive magicians glow with runes that mark the bounties. A frosted-glass wall hides an imposing figure in a high-backed leather chair: the Sheriff of Dusthaven.

His office holds a large mahogany desk, a high-backed leather chair that hovers, and a massive ornate safe under complex wards. On the party's first return the deputy is lazy about it: "Oh hey, y'all are back? Board's over there, pick red, you're dead. Tear one off and good luck."

##### Sheriff of Dusthaven

**GM.** A seasoned lawman, weathered face, long coat hung with magical sigils, an iridescent badge. He looks the party over and does not offer a name anywhere on the sheet. Wyatt Kane is the sheriff of Scorpion Gulch, not of Dusthaven.

##### Gunther's Guns

The hanging sign is metal. Burnt powder comes off the abused range next door. A bell, then a short, stout, brown-haired dwarf pops over the back counter: "Ello folks, look around me shop and lemme know if ya need anything." Cold steel and brass.

**GM.** Gunther is a dwarven man, marked NN on the sheet, with brown hair and a thick beard. He is pleasant until someone makes a rotten offer. He wants money and he wants a fair price. He does not upcharge. Firearms, ammunition, and dynamite on his shelves use the prices in the Firearms compendium. Silvering one weapon or ten pieces of ammunition is 100 gp, and that price is only on his sheet. He also sells firearm accessories and blacksmithing.

##### Spare-a-drop Supplies

Sells adventuring gear and buys whatever. Ordinary goods use the prices in [Mundane goods](#mundane-goods).

**GM.** A looming three-story wooden building. An animated sign shows an oil can pouring a single drop. The porch is jammed with crates and barrels. Inside, the opposite: everything sorted onto tables, shelves, and barrels with prices in front. Springs, a brass warforged, bounces behind the counter and hisses steam at the ceiling.

The General Store sheet repeats this description and does not name Springs. It is this shop, not a second one. Steamwood and Scorpion Gulch have their own general stores, and they sell the same mundane list.

##### The Mayor's House

The office is Elowen's old tower, and the man in it is Thorian Brightshield.

##### Dusthaven Random Encounter Ideas

**GM.** A medicine man's horse is dead and his cart is broken in the desert. If trusted, he says the medicines are expensive and he only needs to get his leather sack home. He offers a dose of the cure-all for an escort toward Dusthaven. Bandits hit them on the way and ask only for the medicine. They leave if they get it. One bandit has a glass bottle of Couch Syrup.

##### Completed bounties

The poster is what the town nailed up.

**Scaly Sam.** Dead or alive. Disturbing the peace, armed robbery, threats. 60 gp. **GM.** Up the abandoned railway, in an abandoned mine. He trained desert lizards and snakes. His famous failure was a robbery with giant poisonous snakes in which he demanded silverware and cash. He fled up the tracks and died trying to train a giant gila monster. The snakes and the gila are still in the cave.

**Axe Beaks.** Dead. An urgent request from Bloomridge. Local artisans need them to assemble and export axes. 10 gp per beak. Bloomridge is the Flameguard camp in the Savagebloom Expanse.

**The Iron Riders.** Dead or alive. Armed robbery, rustling, murder. 200 gp for Varro Blackthorn, their supposed leader. **GM.** Magitech bodies and magitech mounts. Varro is the engineer who builds and maintains the alterations. They camp about a day from town, in a cliff, behind a silent image of solid rock. They killed a rancher's family and took the cattle, which graze just past the gorge.

**Hive Sighting.** Urgent. Remove the hive core. A small hive near Scorpion Gulch, rumored to spawn scorpions. 350 gp. **GM.** Scorpion Gulch is a three-day ride south. The hive is less than a day east of the gulch. The core is a Scorpion Lasher sticking out of the floor by its handle. It attacks anything in reach. A DC 10 Athletics check pulls it free. The weapon made from those pieces is under Tor's gear.

**Supernatural Emergency.** Urgent. Report to the church. A supernatural storm on the frontier. 300 gp. **GM.** Krampus has risen at the Pine family ranch. A blizzard in a perfect one-mile radius: difficult terrain and blindness. After thirty minutes, a DC 14 Constitution save or one level of exhaustion, and the party has reached the house. A frost salamander hides on the roof.

The living room is partly caved in. A shotgun hangs above the hearth: a +1 double-barrel. The possessed corpse nearest it holds 2d6 Permafrost Pellets. Up the chimney, a pouch holds 23 gp, 42 sp, and experimental blueprints for a knuckle shotgun.

The bedroom chest looks like solid ice. Breaking it releases two ice mephits and a pile of common clothes with Frostweaver Gloves on top. The jewelry box on the bedside table is untouched: three silver rings at 15 gp, two silver necklaces at 15 gp, one gold ring at 25 gp, one gold necklace at 25 gp.

Krampus's gate is the sack that opens on the Ninth Circle. That job is Sack of Cold, out of Ironspire.

**Oswald "Friendly Face" Price.** Alive. Counterfeiting, identity theft, fraud, robbery, arson, blackmail, and murder. An escapee from Permafrost Prison, armed and dangerous. 3,500 gp. The poster sits with the completed bounties. Permafrost Prison is on the Frosthook ice.

**Investigate Cemetary.** Spelled that way on the poster. A request from Father Matthews. An acolyte was found dead. The church wants investigative work, not a posted reward. **GM.** Jane Stag was killed on a late round of Boot Hill Cemetery, after recent signs of necromantic ritual. Cedric Brightsoul, a newer acolyte, found her in the morning. He is the person to question.

**Lucky Luke.** Dead or alive. Robbery, adultery, cheating, forgery, and manslaughter. 300 gp. **GM.** He cleaned up at poker. Everyone called it rigged. He called it luck. He beat Butch, which is enough for Butch. He has been shot at and thrown out of saloons. Last seen headed for Steamwood on a train ticket he "found."

The Steamwood chase, if it is used: a log-pile leap at a lumberyard (Athletics DC 15 to make it, Perception DC 12 to spot loose logs, failure costs ground and 1d6 bludgeoning); the marketplace, with no check written down; a bar brawl (Acrobatics DC 12 to dodge, Strength DC 15 to shove through overturned furniture). His face is on the Coin of Divination.

**Marauder Incursion.** Required. A hostage situation. The Marauders have citizens of Dusthaven and serious demands, and they are attacking the northeast edge of town. Dusthaven law, section 3.7, requires engagement. **GM.** This is the same gang as the Sandstorm band and as Tor Longtooth's Marauders. They want the town's information on the Sun Prism. It is their last act as raiders. The party stops the attack, kills Zephyr Scorchblade and Sirocco Emberweaver, and frees the captured civilians. Dusthaven gives the party that information instead. Tor is already one of the band. He does not die here. He follows them afterward.

##### Open bounties

**Shapeshifting Threat.** Alive. Armed robbery, enchantment, murder. The last disguise was not kept. Black ruffled shirt, dark purple vest, black pants, gold accents. 100 gp.

**"Rex Refund".** Alive. Robbery, cheating, forgery, counterfeiting, fraud, and murder. May use other names. Likes fine clothes and wide-brimmed hats. Usually travels with a shirtless, blindfolded hired gun, Scorpion Jack. 500 gp. Bloomridge knows him as Floyd Hibiscus Sr. In 971 he and Scorpion Jack are the party of two that fails to find the Lost City of Gold.

**Creatures.** Bring proof. Giant vulture 5 gp, giant hyena 5 gp, rust monster 3 gp, giant scorpion 10 gp, barroth 20 gp, kulu-ya-ku 20 gp, bulette 25 gp, bone devil 45 gp. Other mana-touched creatures: ask at the desk. This reads as a standing price list, not a finished job.
