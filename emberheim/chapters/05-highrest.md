#### Highrest

The place-name list writes Castle Darkstone in brackets and glosses it Highrest. They are not the same building. Highrest is the new wooden town under the cliff. Castle Darkstone is the black shape above it. The party cleared that castle of Aric, its master, with the Marauders at their back because Tor Longtooth forced the march. Paul Marauder died in the castle. He is buried in Highrest. The party led the Marauders to the cliff, and the town that grew out of that old, shadowed place dubbed the company the Heroes of Highrest. The statue going up in the square commemorates them. The sheets titled Castle Darkstone and Castle Darkstone - Map Overview are blank. So is Castle Darkstone Torture Chamber, Xanthis' Study, and The Heart of Sorrow. What follows is everything the other sheets actually contain.

Castle Darkstone has its own entry. The Cult of Boogle and Xanthis' Tower are in Miscellaneous locations.

**GM.** The path comes out of the mountains and the town is clinging to the cliff under Castle Darkstone. The outer wall is a fresh log palisade, tall, uneven, defiant. Smoke, iron, bread, and leather on a cold wind. A wooden sign in front of a wide log gate reads Highrest. Two armored guards. One steps up: "Halt, what be your business in Highrest, wagon?" The other jolts awake. Full armor clanks forward, and a metallic, reverberating voice comes from inside the helmet.

Past the gate, the bones of an older place: crumbled foundations, half-fallen towers, cobbles split by roots. A medieval village, destroyed and forgotten, is being built again in wood. A smithy, children, traders under mismatched tents. The castle still owns the skyline, like a forgotten god.

Under the overhang, a massive leather pavilion on a wooden frame. Soldiers and townsfolk in and out. The banner is a jagged tooth and a sunburst. That is Tor's tent. At the center of town, a clean new stone fountain. Six figures are still being cut from the stone: four humans, a frog-like creature, and a cat-like construct, side by side, looking up at the castle. Tools and scaffolding. The faces are not finished. People stop anyway, some reverent, some hopeful. The sheet calls this an old town once dead and drowned in shadow.

The six figures are the Heroes of Highrest: Wendy, Az, Khan, and Clint for the four humans, Gulp for the frog, BBQ for the cat-like construct. The sheets never state that assignment in one sentence. Sparky is not on the fountain. Paul's grave is in this town. The Shadow desecrates it.

##### Tor's Tent

**GM.** A young couple comes out smiling. Inside, paperwork and mugs on a table. Tor stands at a topographic map on the tent wall, in his usual armor, a giant sword on his back. It is Aric's blade. He puts a pin in the map, turns, and calls the party his allies. He says they are getting along just peachy, waves, and the armor that walked them in leaves.

This is Tor Longtooth. Torren Blackholt is a different man.

##### The Shadow

All of this sheet is player-visible text. It is the revenge plot after Highrest.

The party returns to Highrest at night. Thick low fog, cold moonlight, quiet streets, few windows still lit. The unfinished fountain statue, their faces just beginning to be cut, is stained wet crimson. Corpses fill the red basin: two men dressed like Az and Khan, one woman dressed like Wendy, and the mangled corpse of a cat and a frog on top. A man in a dark, gold-trimmed cloak floats face down. The cloak is unmistakably Caelric's. Turned over, the face is not Caelric's. It is a random man. Carved into the open chest: SOON.

Up the muddy street, glass breaks and a woman screams. Shadows drag her into the road. She flails. The life drains. Her skin desiccates onto the bone and she stops. Shadows like snakes pour from the alleys, a horde, barely visible by moonlight.

Paul Marauder's grave has been desecrated. A large smiley face is drawn on it in blood. He died in Castle Darkstone and was buried here.

At the inn they were promised, Caelric is gone. His window is shattered. He fought. The room is a mess. Blood, both crimson and blackened. A couple of gold coins near the window. His luggage, books, and paperwork are still there. His coin purse is missing. He cut it open during the fight. Coins are stuck in the mud under the window and lead north out of town.

He is being kept in a cave that was once a mad elemental mage's home. Xanthis and Helena hold it now, for revenge.

A stone golem has become the wall at the entrance. The password is HAIL DARKSTONE. He is semi-intelligent, questions visitors, and has heard of a plot against three humans, a frog, and a cat. If he needs them, he calls galeb duhr.

Inside is the mad mage's house, now Xanthis's laboratory. He imprisoned people here and experimented toward lichdom with the Necronomicon. The mad mage's desiccated body lies in the coffin, hands fastened above the chest, once holding something: a book on elemental rituals.

Books and scrolls on necrotic magic, divine readings, and souls. An Investigation check can find a scroll of tether essence.

A mundanely locked chest, DC 20: ten fine-cut black onyx, two large intricate-cut diamonds, and five stones. A pale lavender ellipsoid, a deep red sphere, a dark blue rhomboid, a pink rhomboid, and a pale green prism.

A dead end to the right has been turned into Helena's resting and torture room. Caelric hangs on a wooden rack, bruised, cut, bloodied, unconscious. A small table of bloodied, rusted tools, and a couple of bloody teeth. A second mundane lock, DC 15: three potions of supreme healing, five of superior healing, a scroll of speak with dead, and a pearly white spindle-shaped stone.

Helena waits in the shadows and strikes as soon as the party enters, aiming to kill the light first. Between rounds she talks:

- "I am Helena Darkstone, daughter of Aric Darkstone. You killed my father, prepare to die."
- "I have awaited this day. I have joined in the crowds and watched you all. I did not expect you to return."
- "I will make sure that your deaths are painful, and that Xanthis gets each one of your souls. Except for the new guy. He seems claimed."
- "Once we're done here, your froggy friend comes next."

After a couple of rounds, Xanthis teleports in. A pure black doorway opens. He steps out, more of a shambling corpse than when the party threw him off the cliffs of Darkstone. "I RETURN, FOOLS! Stop your playing, Helena, it's almost complete. BEGONE!" He drags her through a dimensional door to a point above the mountain.

Several rounds later the elemental ritual detonates. An earthquake. The mountain seems to rise, then lurches, taking large slow steps. It is a zaratan, headed for Highrest. A modified devastation orb has gone off at an elemental node on the mountain's tip. The quake is felt a mile away in Highrest. Xanthis wears a Ring of Elemental Command (earth) to keep the beast on route. He hovers above the tip. Helena stands under him.

When the party comes out of the cavern she shouts down: "Everything you created will be destroyed, along with so many lives. Enjoy the show before your deaths." Then she backs into the shadows.

Krieg's warning about a corrupted granddaughter matches Helena. Lady Evelina Darkstone has nothing written, and she is not the same woman.
