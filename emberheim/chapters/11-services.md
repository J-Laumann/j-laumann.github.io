## Shared services

The Railway Station, the Stables, and the Church serve more than one town. Dusthaven and Steamwood use all three. Anchorfall uses the church and the stables, and it is not on the railway.

### The Stables

Hitching posts, hay and manure, locked gates into a yard. A burly man behind the counter: "Thanks for the coming to the stables folks, looking to buy or house?" A giant lizard is 50 gp. An axe beak is 50 gp. He says to ask about anything else.

### Railway Station

Public trains run three lines. A fourth run goes to Fort Ironclad from Ironspire or from Steamwood, and that trip is private, only for soldiers of the Empire. Anchorfall is not on the timetable.

The train is the coal steamer at 10 miles an hour. At that pace almost every ride is a day of travel, and the hours below are that day. The fare is about 8 cp a mile. Eight copper times 125 miles is 10 gp exactly. Eight copper times 80 miles is 6 gp 4 sp, and that fare is rounded down to 6 gp. Eight copper times 65 miles is 5 gp 2 sp. Eight copper times 50 miles is 4 gp. Mundane is the other end of the Glacier's Edge line.

| Line | Miles | Time | Fare | Who may ride |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dusthaven and Steamwood | 125 | a day, 12 hours 30 minutes | 10 gp | Public |
| Ironspire and Steamwood | 80 | a day, 8 hours | 6 gp | Public |
| Mundane and Glacier's Edge | 125 | a day, 12 hours 30 minutes | 10 gp | Public |
| Steamwood and Fort Ironclad | 65 | a day, 6 hours 30 minutes | 5 gp 2 sp | Soldiers of the Empire |
| Ironspire and Fort Ironclad | 50 | a day, 5 hours | 4 gp | Soldiers of the Empire |

### The Church

The same rites as the capital church through Raise Dead, without Regenerate and Resurrection. Any spell it does sell uses the Ironspire price.
