#### Ironspire

Ironspire is the capital: constant invention, metal, and mana. The Empire protects its citizens here with breakthroughs in condensed mana. Magitech inventors run through a city that seems made of metal and steam. Emberheim exports creations and imports raw materials. It is a hungry town.

Most buildings are metallic spears shoved up through the steam that clings to the streets like fog. None of them may rise above the Imperial Palace. The Council meets there to pass laws, order the military, and approve new inventions.

The stolen schematic, the gigs, the sack of cold, the contest, and the audience are in Weird West.

##### Imperial Palace

The center of government. The Council of Emberheim passes the major laws. Seven chairs:

| Seat | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Scales | Overseer of Trade |
| Ship | Overseer of Transportation |
| Open Book | Overseer of the Arcane |
| Gear | Overseer of Innovation |
| Tree | Overseer of Land |
| Cross | Overseer of Faith |
| Swords | Overseer of War |

**GM, from Official Business.** A Bronze Hero Badge hums and shocks in the pocket when Torren Blackholt calls. He waits at the palace doors, hands behind his back, and says the empire has a small request, and that a couple of church figures wanted to catch the heroes while they were in the building.

The lobby is about thirty feet high. A thin waterfall glosses a brass map of Emberheim on the left. Another waterfall covers seven brass icons, the council seats. Stone and brass run to stairs. Guards on the ground floor ignore Torren and the party. A gnome in religious garb waves. Saint Nicholas stands beside him.

Nicholas thanks them for the church, congratulates them on becoming heroes, and says he knew they could do it. The gnome thanks them for the people they saved and for what the church got out of the Velkhana crystal. He returns the crystal. The research confirms they entered the Ninth Circle of Hell and fought one of its guardians. The notes themselves will not help much. He wants someone capable to look at the crystal: it is a serious source of magical power and could make fantastic gear. Nicholas adds that the badges make it much easier to convince the church to hire them, then leaves.

Torren takes them up in a brass cage. Levers and a button. Several floors up he opens double doors and announces the young heroes. Six figures sit on slightly raised podiums. The sheet ends there. Faith's seat is the empty one: Archbishop Evalyne is marked Missing, which leaves six councilors in the room.

##### The overseers

**Trade, Orwin Delvane.** A portly man in his mid-fifties, neatly trimmed goatee, robe lined with gold thread. He always smells faintly of cologne and ink. His rings jingle when he signs paperwork. Sheet: Trade - Orwin Delvane.

**Transportation, Essira Tidewake.** A lean water genasi woman with light-blue skin and long white hair in a single braid. She wears a weather-beaten naval coat pinned with compasses. She leans back in her chair and would put her muddy boots on the table if anyone allowed it. Sheet: Transportation - Essira Tidewake. The Flying Boats sheet Essira Tidewake repeats this description and adds nothing.

**Arcane, Vaelrix Morra.** An ageless, regal elven man with luminous violet eyes and layered robes inscribed with glyphs. His voice carries an aged weight. His left hand stays in a silk glove and the glove never comes off. **GM.** He was born in Aetheria more than six hundred years ago. Sheet: Arcane - Vaelrix Morra.

**Innovation, Fenwick Sprox.** A middle-aged gnome. Blond hair slicked back with streaks of silver, silver goggles over a blue left eye and a brown right eye. A mechanical arm rests on the table under a stained leather coat. Sheet: Innovation - Fenwick Sprox.

**Land, Bren Tallwood.** He looks ancient: towering, calm, moss in the beard, bark-like skin along the arms. A long green cloak clasped with antler bone. He speaks slowly, as if weighing every word like stone. Sheet: Land - Bren Tallwood.

**Faith, Archbishop Evalyne.** The player note is one word: Missing. Sheet: Faith - Archbishop Evalyne.

**War, Kael Durnhart.** A broad-shouldered dwarf in ceremonial steel even in council. His beard is braided into three points tipped with iron clasps. His voice is gravel on steel. A sword leans on every table he sits at. Sheet: War - Kael Durnhart.

##### Brass & Barter

Sells adventuring gear, food and drink, tools, blacksmithing, medicine, and potions. Ordinary gear, tools, and trade goods use the prices in [Mundane goods](#mundane-goods).

Steam hisses when the door opens. Clockwork hums. The place smells of polished metal and oiled leather. Dark wood shelves with brass fittings hold rations and gadgets. A brass chandelier turns overhead. Automaton assistants click between the aisles. The counter shows high-end clockwork, with a sturdy brass scale for supplies and haggling. Behind it, a wall of gears carries the mark: a cogwheel twisted with a coin.

##### The Grease Trap

**GM.** Under the streets, down a slick iron stair, the air is sweet smoke and spilled ale. The underground room mixes low talk, glass, and the occasional fist. Soot, oil, a sticky floor, furniture that looks salvaged from scrap: mismatched tables, dented mugs, a bar held together with rivets and glue. Ruffians dice in the corner. Another table plans a score. Tobacco drifts from an alcove where a cloaked figure watches.

##### Briggs' BOOM!

Sells firearms, dynamite, ammunition, and accessories. Experimental ammunition, in compact, medium, or shell, is 25 gp. Long experimental rounds are 30 gp. The named experimental loads are the Heart Seeker Bullet, the Flak Shell, and the Lightning Rod Bullet.

**GM.** Gunpowder, oil, and hot metal. The room is a chaos of parts, tools, and guns under warm lamps. Behind a cluttered counter, a gnome with wild gray hair squints at a pistol and talks to himself. His mechanical arm clicks, screwdriver-hand on a bolt. Something behind the counter thumps like a clock. Muffled gunfire comes through the wall and he ignores it. A sign says "Test Before You Buy" and points left. His line: "If it's got a trigger, we've got it, or I'll build it." The named sheet gives him the full name Briggs Magren. The sheet titled Briggs has no other text.

##### Crystals and Curios

**GM.** A bell, then old parchment, polished brass, and something arcane. Enchanted gadgets hum on cramped shelves. Gears and oddities cover the walls in an organized chaos. Clive Orecrusher sits on a worn stool behind the counter, beard grayer than when the party last saw him in Scorpion Gulch. He still wears the steel mining helmet with the monocle flip-downs, the helmet of identification from his family's hall. He buys and sells magic items here because that helmet makes identification easy. He looks up from a mechanical bird: "Well, look who finally wandered over. What brings you lot to my little corner of Ironspire?"

##### The Gilded Gear

Rooms, per day: basic 8 sp, fancy 2 gp, penthouse 7 gp. All-inclusive: basic 2 gp, fancy 4 gp, penthouse 10 gp.

**GM.** Warm light, money, exotic incense, expensive liquor. A vast room of talk. Decorative brass gears turn in the walls and ceiling. Polished granite is inlaid with gold. Card tables, roulette, dice pits, an automaton pianist, bartenders in waistcoats with jewel-colored drinks. Balconies like opera boxes, lanterns, inn rooms behind frosted glass. High-roller rooms sit behind doorways of shrouded fog, several floors up. At the door, a humming bronze security arch ripples like liquid metal, and a uniformed attendant nods. Several floors up, Sorrin Quill leans on a railing and waves a black-feathered hand. See his entry under People.
