### The Elemental Plane of Air


Carter Wint opened this road. In 973 he found a Node of Air in the Sea of Sand. In 974 he met the Aarakocra, learned the name Yan-C-Bin, and started bringing Auran work home. In 975 Yan-C-Bin attacked Shakharra, killed many, and possessed him. Carter retreated to his castle, and from that year Yan-C-Bin has a physical body. The present company sees the cloud islands on summer 21–22, before they ever reach Evershroud.

#### Shakharra

The only remaining town of the Aarakocra, and it stands in the Elemental Plane of Air. A fortress on a gargantuan floating island. Wooden docks ring a small harbor on the south peninsula. Floating wooden carts are parked along those docks. Lord Kaarak rules it. His name is said kay-rack. The Flying Boats company only glimpses the place during the summer of the cloud islands. In 975 Yan-C-Bin attacked it, killed many, and possessed Carter Wint there.

#### Lord Kaarak's Questions

**GM.** Each character is taken down into a room of nondetection. The only interview written out is McMasterson's, under People. At the end of it, the way back is to reenter the same node, show the sigil, and meet Kaarak here. The page does not say the sigil was handed over.

#### CW's Hidden Laboratory

Hidden deep underwater. Carter can reach it because he does not need to breathe. A huge stone door sits at the bottom of a deep lake, barely lit, the letters CW carved into algae-ridden stone. The last key was entrusted to Lord Kaarak.

The key touches the door and the stone becomes a light yellow force field. Through it, a large dry room. Glowing orbs line the walls, low and jarring, and the light leaks into the water. Walking through drops a person to the floor. Buoyancy is gone. Clothes and body are dry. The stone is roughly carved into a huge circle, with alcoves of tables, chairs, and shelves: metal, stone, crystal, and dozens of books.

#### First Node

Three islands, reached from a node.

**Mined island.** Small, mostly crag. Footing on the north end, then a tunnel to an open quarried middle. A flooded cavern goes deeper, propped with old water-damaged wood. Anything wild in there collapses it. A feral water elemental lives in the water. At the top of the caverns, a small wooden room. The furniture and papers are waterlogged and strewn. One note is hammered above the door. One chunk of Aureleonite is under the dirty water in that room. The sheet spells it Aurelionite. More could be mined deeper in, if the cavern holds.

**Couatl island.** A snaking island, lush on top, two small crags floating beside it. A river winds through the middle, drops down short cliffs, and falls off into mist. Couatls fly lazy between the trees and above them. Some rush in, curious, shapeshift to mimic the company for fun, and mutter Auran in their minds. Behind the tallest waterfall is the nest. Among the sleeping Couatls, piles of sticks, grasses, and leaves hold silver, gold, and crystal. If Wint is mentioned, they fetch a stone orb with white glowing lines spiraling on it.

**Node island.** Large, sparse forest and tall grass, then a sheer cliff. Below the cliff the rock is worked stone, and a huge double door is set in the wall. A small rounded basin stands in front. Smooth twirling runes arch over the doors: "Unlock these doors only if you wish to guard this portal with your life."

The key goes in. White light climbs the walls and the seam. The doors swing forward on a gust of stale air. Inside, a circular room carved in the island, a soft white glow between the floor tiles. Skeletons slump along the walls in tattered rags, old spears in their hands. They were not human. Each skull has a large beak, and bony wings lie behind them. One wears armor and sits a stone throne across the room. The doors begin to shut.

The skeletons rise and do not advance. They ready their weapons. A moment of stillness, then they charge. When the last bones drop, white light surges through the floor. The thick stone column in the center hums, shoots a beam into the ceiling, and the beam hits a giant crystal, flies into the doors, and pushes them open. It dissipates into clouds a little off the island. The clouds congeal, become a crystalline mass, and shatter outward into a rapidly spinning circle. Through it: a rough sea, a low fog, and many small lights in the distance.

As the ship pushes for the portal, figures rush past from inside the island. Masses of cloud, faces darkened with anger, and in front a human in gleaming metal armor. Streaks of white light swirl on his flesh. He flies with nothing holding him up, fists forward, one hand on a longsword, and he looks back grinning. His eyes are glowing white. They go straight for the portal.

On the far side the cloudy creatures shrink. Some pop into several smaller ones. Only the largest two keep their shape. The humanoid slows, staggers in the air, and the light in him dulls. He keeps on, straight for the lights.

That crossing is the summer of 21–22. The ritual is beaten, a node home opens for a moment, and the Auran agent who comes through near Azure Lagoon is killed at once. He names Yan-C-Bin before he dies.

#### Yan-C-Bin

His possession of Carter, the army, and the storm are under People. The order of the war is this. A minor victory in 970 leaks magic into the physical plane and thickens Evershroud. The attack on Shakharra is 975. The agent at Azure Lagoon is the summer the company opens the node home. He is still trying to come through the strongest node he can find.
