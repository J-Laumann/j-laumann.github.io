## The West

The Sea of Sand and Heaven's Reach: Dusthaven, Scorpion Gulch, Highrest, and Castle Darkstone. Three groups belong to that ground.

### Heroes of Highrest

The Weird West company, dubbed that after they defeat Aric and lead the Marauders to the cliff. The town that grows there is Highrest, founded in 971. The statue being cut in the square is theirs: Wendy, Az, Khan, and Clint, Gulp the frog, BBQ the construct. Sparky is not on it. Tor Longtooth is not one of the six. He left them, and he is the man who later keeps the tent in that town.

### The Marauders

One gang. Zephyr Scorchblade and Sirocco Emberweaver lead them when they are a small band of desert raiders, masks and stolen mounts and a limestone cave, with a train from Ironspire to Dusthaven still on their map. Tor Longtooth leaves the Heroes of Highrest and joins that band. He unifies it around a home for downcast people with dark pasts. Their last raid is Dusthaven, to steal the town's information on the Sun Prism. The company kills Zephyr and Sirocco, frees the captives, and is paid with that information instead. Tor follows in secret, takes the prism at the Sunfire Temple, and forces the company north to Castle Darkstone. After Aric falls, the company leads the Marauders to Highrest. That is the home. Paul Marauder dies in the castle and is buried there.

Kentucky James, a tomb robber of the band, is already dead in the temple when the company reaches him. His Dusthaven bounty is still written open. The early plan to rob the Ironspire train has no outcome.

### House Darkstone

The old royal house of the castle above Highrest. King Krieg von Darkstone waits in the tomb. Queen Beatrice is his wife, and she is not Beatrice Horner of the Lounging Lizard. Aric, their records' fallen prince, killed her in a rage because she was not his birth mother. The Heroes of Highrest clear the castle of him. Helena is his daughter. She is still at large when Weird West stops: Caelric on a rack, Paul's grave painted with a bloody smile, and a zaratan Xanthis has woken and pointed at the town. Xanthis was thrown from the cliffs, came back more corpse than man, and is chasing lichdom. Lady Evelina Darkstone's sheet is blank, and she is not folded into Helena.
