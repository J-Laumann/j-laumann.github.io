#### Castle Darkstone

From Castle Darkstone - Areas. The keys are the sheet's own room numbers.

**K15, Chapel.** Dim colored light through tall stained glass. A dome ninety feet up. A balcony along the west wall, fifty feet above the floor. Something dark and slumped in a tall chair at the balcony's center. Benches coated with bodies, in disarray. Beyond them, a shaft of light on an altar. The altar's sides are angels entwined with grape vines. A silver statuette stands in the light. A figure in a white cloak is draped over the altar. A black mace lies near its feet.

If a character handles the statuette, the figure on the balcony calls down. One line if it judges them tainted: "A valiant attempt, seems as though sin weighs on you as well, feel free to set what remains of them with the others." One line if it judges them decent: "A good-natured man? Thank you for ridding us of that spiteful relic. Please do set it somewhere less, in the way, before you die."

**K25, Audience Hall.** Dim courtyard light through a broken west window of glass and iron lattice. Empty sconces. Cobwebs hiding the ceiling. Double doors in the east wall, opposite the window, and a single door farther south on that same wall. Stairs at both ends of the north wall go down. At the south end, a wooden throne on a marble dais faces south, toward a portrait of a young woman, away from most of the room. Behind the portrait: a fancy crimson handkerchief (Her Handkerchief, later in Az's gear), an opened bottle of fine red wine that is poisoned, and a gold ring set with fine-cut diamonds, 500 gp.

**K41, Treasury.** The tower's bottom floor is a hoard: at least 10,000 sp and 10,000 gp, a few glints of platinum, and 15 assorted gems. On the pile, a silver shield with a wailing face. The upper floor has a 6-by-9-foot carpet of flying and a table. The jewelry box holds ten pieces of fine jewelry at 250 gp each, an amulet of health, and an alchemy jug.

**K42, King's bedchamber.** Sweet smells, heavy red drapes with gold tassels on a great arched west window, three candelabras of tall white candles. A canopied bed against the north wall. The headboard is a rising or setting sun, carved with skill. A beautiful young woman in a nightgown lies in the velvet and satin. She is Aric's lost love, Valentina, and she is an illusion. She can be seen only through the bed's curtains. One half of the bed is well used. The other is dusty.

**K44, Closet.** Investigating the racks springs the wight that burst from the tub. An iron key in a coat pocket opens the iron door to the dungeon.

**K78, Brazier room.** Cast a stone into the fire.

##### Putting Beatrice to Rest

A hushed tomb. Tall stained glass on the east walls. Dim light on two coffins on white marble. The north coffin is marked King Krieg von Darkstone. The south coffin is marked "Queen Bea" and the rest is scratched out. The vaulted ceiling, thirty feet up, is an inlaid gold mosaic. A tall ethereal man stands with his hands behind his back, looking down and out through the glass. "It's beautiful, isn't it. The people finally return to village Darkstone." He turns: an aged, full face, a small smile.

His coffin holds a hollow wax replica of him at death, eyes closed, uncannily real. His wife's coffin holds a tattered white shroud and traces of dust.

He is glad to see the village alive again, and glad his son's torturous existence is over. He thanks the party if they admit they ended it. He asks one more thing. Aric killed his wife in a rage. Aric had always despised her for not being his birth mother. Her body is beneath her resting place, behind the struck-out plaque. Krieg wants the bones placed properly in the casket, and the plaque repaired if they can, so her soul can go with his. He has promised to wait for eternity. He warns that she has had a long time to grow angry in that small compartment, and that the party may do what they must, including striking her ghastly figure down.

If they do as asked, Beatrice reforms, unmarred, says nothing, and takes Krieg's hand. They look back. He tells the party to look within his heart for a proper reward, thanks them, and asks that this land never fall into corruption again. He adds that he is afraid the soul of his granddaughter still walks, twisted by corruption, and to look out for her. Then the two of them fade through the stained glass into a twinkle of moonlight.

Breaking the wax, brittle old bones. Fallen from the crumbling spine and cradled in the chest cavity: a glimmering platinum amulet, the Holy Symbol of Darkstone. The granddaughter is Helena, from The Shadow, though this sheet does not speak her name.
