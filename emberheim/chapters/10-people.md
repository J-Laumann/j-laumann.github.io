## People

The fountain at Highrest is cutting six figures: four humans, a frog, and a cat-like construct. Wendy, Az, Khan, and Clint are the four points of view at the Contest. Gulp's gear is a monk's kit, and Helena calls the frog the next target. BBQ is the construct Wendy is measured against, and a cat's corpse is piled with the frog's in The Shadow. After they defeated Aric and led the Marauders to the cliff, the town that grew there dubbed them the Heroes of Highrest. The square's statue is theirs. Sparky is carried as a separate inventory and is not on the fountain.

Tor Longtooth left this company, took the Marauders in hand, and later compelled them north to Castle Darkstone. Highrest is the home he wanted for them. He is a seventh person, not one of the six statues. Xanthis' tower tells the party to find him before they loot the bedroom.

### Sparky

The whole sheet is a carry list. Limit 480. Fifty feet of rope at 5 pounds, and a bedroll at 7 pounds. No portrait, no class, no other mention.

### BBQ

BBQ Inventory: capacity 210. A +1 shield at 6 pounds. Raw duergar gold, spelled "Duerger" on the sheet, at 10 pounds. A crowbar. A harmonica. Miscellaneous items of Khan's, about 20 pounds. Eighteen rations at 36 pounds. The Thighbone of Disruption at 4 pounds, which is the Mace of Disruption filed under BBQ's items. A Silver Helmet of Three Faces at 4 pounds. The helmet has no item sheet. At the Contest, Wendy's attention goes to a steel construct "much like BBQ," so BBQ is the party's machine, and the cat in the fountain and in the fountain-massacre is the same shape of body.

### Wendy

Iron Spire Academy, same class as Lysander Malcove. She notices constructs first. Her item folder holds the Sanctum Amulet, a +1 Crystal, the Amulet of Shifting Seaweed, the Revenue Rifle, and the Staff of the Cauldron Carrier. The seaweed amulet and the staff both came out of Aetherian treasure: the kelpie's lair, and the Orecrusher display case. The rifle is the weapon that pays her in gold when she rolls a 20.

### Az

Dusthaven bounty hunter before the party. The Graves siblings used to snipe contracts out from under him, then left for Steamwood. His folder is the long one: Heaven's Lance, the Henry Stormshot, the Shard of Warmth, the Bottle of Boundless Coffee, Hunter's Best Buddy, the Double Jumpers, the Hunter's Coat, the Lantern of Truth, Scale-shot, the Opportune Uppercut, the Lucky Lid, the Chains of Ethereal Torment, and Her Handkerchief. The handkerchief is the crimson one behind the portrait in Castle Darkstone's audience hall, and it still smells of apricots. The shard is the warmth the Ninth Circle's giants are starving for. Heaven's Lance is priced, on its own sheet, at 12,000 gold.

### Khan

Another Dusthaven bottom-dweller who has been away from Ironspire long enough that the old faces are a surprise. He is one of the very few people who knows Sorrin Quill's face. In the Ninth Circle, the distant beast is written as landing on him specifically. His folder: the Marksman's Cloak, the Phantomstrike Rifle, Boots of Elvenkind, Sporosylvan, the Buckler of Bone, the Pretty Pistol, Goggles of Night, Delectable Dice, the Coin of Divination, Eyes of the Eagle, the Infernal Fang Dagger, and a Luck Blade. The boots were on the dead halfling under the Altar of Rot. The goggles were in Sunfire Temple, area 17. The coin is Lucky Luke's smirk. The dagger and a storage key were in the right arm of the Shattered Colossus. The key was not given its own sheet.

### Clint

The fourth human at the Contest, the one surprised by Haruki and Talia. Helena, torturing Caelric, calls someone "the new guy" and says he seems claimed. The sheet does not name Clint in that sentence. His folder is the warlock's kit: Ring of Demonic Dominance, the Ring of Y'ghnath'ull, a Ring of Spell Storing, the Wand of Secrets, a Rod of the Pact Keeper (+1), the Mind Sharpener, the Soul Orb, a Robe of Useful Items, an Animated Shield, a Luckstone, the Holy Symbol of Darkstone, the Skull of Stefan Gregorovich, and the Skull of Intree Sik-Valoo. The holy symbol came out of Krieg's wax chest. The skulls are Darkstone dead. The Soul Orb was in the Colossus's right arm. The luckstone was in Sunfire area 17.

### Gulp

No scene calls him by name. The item folder and the frog on the fountain are the evidence. His kit is a monk's: Manual of Bodily Health, the Boogle Ki Ring, a Cloak of Protection, the Pocket Anchor, the Kulu Cross, a Ring of Protection, Shotgun Knuckles, and the Blessing of Holy Hands. Qesnef in Sunfire area 27 also wore a ring of protection. The sheet does not say Gulp's ring is that ring. The Kulu Cross throws a boulder and lets the user ride it. Kulu-ya-ku are a 20 gp proof-bounty out of Dusthaven, and Scale-shot, in Az's kit, coughs their feathers.

### Tor Longtooth

Brown scales, reptilian eyes. The sheets also call him Tor. He left the party because he could not stand their tolerance of a heathen and of necromancers, and he joined the Marauders while Zephyr Scorchblade and Sirocco Emberweaver still led that small desert band. He unified them around a home for downcast people with dark pasts. After the Dusthaven fight he followed the party in secret to the Sunfire Temple, took the Sun Prism, and forced them to work with him. He made them lead the way north of Dusthaven to Castle Darkstone. In Highrest, the town that grew after that march, he wears his usual armor, carries Aric's blade, and works under a tooth-and-sunburst banner. His tent holds a topographic map stuck with pins. One of his armored guards speaks with a metallic voice from inside the helm, and Tor greets the party there as allies. Torren Blackholt is a different man.

The bedroom chest in Xanthis' tower tells the party to find Tor before they take the coins. His item folder is the captain's share of the campaign: the purified Sword of Aric, the sentient ??? Sword, a Carpet of Flying, the Shield of Whispered Warnings, the Icon of Faith, Boots of Levitation, the Sandstorm Cloak, the Barroth Scalp Helm, Barroth Ridge Armor, the Ring of Seething, the Scorpion Lasher, and the Sun Prism. The lasher is the Dusthaven hive core, pulled out of the floor. The ring is the cracked silver band from the Orecrusher vault. Castle Darkstone's treasury has a 6-by-9 carpet of flying. His sheet does not say that carpet is his.

### Torren Blackholt

A retired military leader and the new Leader of the Heroes of Emberheim. Tall, broad-shouldered, black hair swept back, blackened plate, a massive shield. He runs the Contest, he calls the badges, and he walks the heroes into the palace. The Bronze Hero Badge is one of his scales. He is an ancient bronze dragon, using the badge as a shock-line and as a one-time revivify. See Ironspire. His Heroes of Emberheim are the empire's titled heroes. The company that founded Highrest is the Heroes of Highrest. He is not Tor Longtooth.

### Saint Nicholas

Hired through a miserable amount of paperwork, lost to Krampus, recovered as a corpse from the Ninth Circle along with others, after the party killed Velkhana. He revives, finds them a week later, thanks them, briefs them, and tells them to become official heroes. He is at the palace door with an unnamed gnome when the badges first call them in. The church's research on the crystal is the gnome's. Nicholas says the badges make the church easier to hire.

### Elowen Sunweaver

**GM.** An elven archmage of fire and sun, and now a lich. She founded Dusthaven in 906, and she created the temple that housed the Sun Prism. She lives deep in the Enigma, where time does not move on her, and she answers to Newole so the fae cannot take the name Elowen. The world does not know that she founded Dusthaven, does not know where she is, and does not know she is a lich. Those three things have always been kept secret. What the Weird West company learned is narrower: she built that temple, and she is a powerful wizard. Dusthaven's own sheet tells a different story, a human explorer and a mana oasis. That is the story the town has. Her old tower is the mayor's office, and her sealed journals are still in it. Her voice in the Pool of Radiance asks the prism's finders to come to the Enigma and ask for Newole. Entry 37 of Elowen's Journal is the riddle map. Her home sheet has no room description beyond that refuge.

### Thorian Brightshield

Current mayor of Dusthaven. A wise, stoic paladin who has served the town for decades and is now old. He means to protect the people and the town's secrets. His "Letter Opener" is a Sparks rifle rebuilt as a one-handed weapon: a straight stock, and metal under the barrel sharpened to an edge. Close problems and far problems, with the same gun. The mayor's building is Elowen's tower.

### Aric Darkstone

Sir Aric the Fallen, son of King Krieg. Beatrice was not his birth mother, and he killed her in a rage. His existence after that is the "torturous" one Krieg is glad to hear has ended. His lost love Valentina is an illusion in the king's bed, visible only through the curtains. His blade is the giant sword on Tor Longtooth's back. The purified sword in Tor Longtooth's folder is the Sword of Aric. Helena is his daughter.

### Helena Darkstone

Daughter of Aric. She watched the party from inside crowds and did not expect them to return to Highrest. She and Xanthis take Caelric to the mad mage's cave. She drains a woman in the street, desecrates Paul Marauder's grave with a bloody smiley face, and leaves SOON carved in a stranger wearing Caelric's cloak. She fights in the dark, puts out lights, and promises the souls to Xanthis, except "the new guy," who seems claimed. The frog is next. When the zaratan wakes she tells the town, from the mountain, that everything they built will be destroyed. Krieg does not know her name. He fears a granddaughter still walking, twisted by corruption.

### Lady Evelina Darkstone

She is not Helena.

### King Krieg von Darkstone and Queen Beatrice

Krieg waits in the tomb under the gold mosaic, glad the village lives and glad his son is done. He asks the party to put Beatrice's bones in her coffin and repair the scratched plaque. She may have to be fought. If the work is done, she reforms unhurt, takes his hand, and they leave through the stained glass. The reward in his heart is the Holy Symbol of Darkstone. Stefan Gregorovich was his first counselor. Intree Sik-Valoo was a sage of that age. Both skulls talk, and both are in Clint's kit. Queen Beatrice the dead queen is not Beatrice Horner of the Lounging Lizard.

### Xanthis

General Xanthis the Necromancer animates the dead in a riverside tower, keeps a laboratory of reversed potions, and was thrown off the cliffs of Darkstone by the party. He comes back more corpse than man, chasing lichdom through the Necronomicon in the mad elemental mage's cave. Helena is his partner until he is done playing. He owns a Ring of Elemental Command (earth). He wakes a mountain as a zaratan and aims it at Highrest. Xanthis' Study, inside Castle Darkstone, is unwritten. The brazier room's red stone is supposed to lead to a study numbered K37. The tower and The Shadow are where his work is written.

### Caelric and Paul

Caelric is taken from the Highrest inn. He fought. Crimson blood and blackened blood. He cut his own purse so the coins would leave a trail north. Helena has him on a rack in the cave, teeth on the side table. She calls him, or the person with the party who is not one of the old targets, the new guy. The sheet does not sort those two cleanly.

Paul Marauder died in Castle Darkstone while the party cleared it of Aric. He was close to them. He rests in Highrest, and the growing town keeps his grave. Helena desecrates it with a bloody smile. He has no sheet of his own.

### Val and Valentina

Valentina is Aric's illusion in the bed, a young woman in a nightgown, apricots still in the handkerchief behind her portrait. Val appears only in the succubus dream in the Ninth Circle: a habit, a cross, a prayer, and the dream's attention. They are left as two names.

### Boogle

The vampire under the mountains. See the cult under Highrest. The Boogle Ki Ring is a monk's item in Gulp's folder: an action to regain ki equal to one roll of the Martial Arts die, once a day. The sheet spells the type line "Wonderoud Item."

### Clive Orecrusher

A dwarf who once lived in Scorpion Gulch and appraised ore at the Miner's Union. The Heroes of Highrest helped him reclaim his family's old hall. The helm on the vault pedestal is a helmet of identification, a steel miner's hat with monocle flip-downs. It let him move up. He now buys and sells magic items at Crystals and Curios in Ironspire, where identification is easy work. The shop sheet never prints the surname. See Scorpion Gulch and Ironspire.

Owner of the Axeman's Alehouse. The portrait is under Steamwood.


### Sorrin Quill

Black-feathered Aarakocra. One sheet spelled him Sorin. In the years Khan was a bottom-dweller, the city knew this person as the Dagger of the People: blackmail for corrupt nobles, death for lawless judges. Almost nobody knows the face or the name. Khan does. At the Gilded Gear he leans over a high railing and waves down.

### Will

The researcher who took the Velkhana crystal at 250 gp a week and was to return it with the findings. No description. The gnome at the palace is the one who hands the crystal back and explains it. The sheet does not say the gnome is Will.

### The Flying Boats company

Nine years after Weird West. McMasterson captains the expedition and speaks for Emberheim. Kincaid Wint, Kayren, Borin Pyre, and Winston Bor are the company the sheets equip. Elric Caltor travels with the charge to watch Kincaid. Their hold list, their night in the manor, and the Plane of Air are in the chapters after the relics.

### McMasterson

**GM.** Lord Kaarak questions him alone, in a room of nondetection, and begins with a staredown that is only an insight check on emotion. McMasterson gives his name again. He represents the people of Emberheim. He wants to be someone important, and he wants to be the one who opens relations. He is captain of the ship they travel on, and he says they have one in Emberheim as well. He leads the expedition into the Plane of Air. He met the others recently. They seem to value coin, they are being paid, and he does not trust them past that. Combat is not his strength, though he has been in it and can hold his own, and he says the same of his companion. He finds Kaarak cautious, rightly so, caring of his people, and welcoming. Kaarak had said that trust could be marked with a sigil. McMasterson asks how to come back and negotiate. The answer on the sheet is to reenter the same node, show the sigil, and meet Kaarak there. The sheet does not say the sigil was given.

### Kincaid Wint

Mad Mist gives him vivid hallucinations and disadvantage on ability checks. At the manor dinner he takes salad, and he is the only one served pork. His gear is the Floab Lance, the Powder Horn of Pazazz, the Pixie Scissors, the Spell Sewing Needle, the Pipe of Blowing Smoke, and the Bangles of Auran Armory. The timeline says he eventually holds the Aurelic Crystal Carter left at Evershroud. That crystal has no item sheet. Elric Caltor was sent to watch him.

### Kayren

Mad Mist makes him repeat small acts three times: washing, counting coins, touching things. Mister made him a spider-silk scarf. His other gear is a Philter of Love, the Safety Bear, the Candle of Borrowed Shadows, the Bark of Debarking, the Flute of the Fae, and the Pocket Cleric. At dinner he takes soup, with Winston, and beef with the rest.

**GM.** Kayren and his family were killed in 97?. An Auran agent took his life. The player of that agent is Nash. The faceless nightmares are Auran. The agent was once like those nightmares, was pushed through a node only barely, and was ordered to kill the family and take a place of power.

### Borin Pyre

The only one of the company Crazy Fog Man says was not infected with Mad Mist. He discovered the Pearlob. At dinner he takes salad with Kincaid, and beef. His gear is the Floab ore, the Gauntlets of Unyielding Grasp, a Pearl of Power, and a +1 Floab Greatsword.

### Winston Bor

Bugbear blood, from the burst of magic at the Enigma. The mist at Evershroud is that same magic mixed with a Node of Air, and it takes him harder than the others. During Party Night the company eases the change. He becomes a full fae. The crowd of fae leaving the manor, rowdy on their way out, whisks him back to the Enigma. He carries the Amulet of Last Whispers. At dinner he takes soup.

### Elric Caltor

**GM.** A Couatl in disguise. His sheet writes the people as coatls. In 978 the Couatls sent him through an outward node they still hold, a last attempt to find help against Yan-C-Bin. Their ritual opened the inward node to the south and began Flying Boats. Carter did not send him. Nothing here says the company knows what he is.

### Carter Wint

Air genasi. He does not need to breathe, which is how he reaches the laboratory under the lake. In 973 he found a Node of Air in the Sea of Sand. In 974 he held the fog back at Evershroud, left a barrier in the church and an Aurelic Crystal, met The Lady of the House through a node, and began learning Auran work from the Aarakocra. In 975 Yan-C-Bin possessed him during the attack on Shakharra and drove him back to his castle. The body is the body Yan-C-Bin wears. The quarry note is signed C. W. The GM line under that note is the one word Carter.

### Yan-C-Bin

**GM.** He has taken physical form in Carter Wint's body. He is using Carter's air genasi blood and Carter's magic to build an army of Auran constructs. He has taken the Plane of Air and wrapped it in storm. He is putting down Aarakocra and Couatl resistance, and he is trying to enter the physical plane through the strongest node he can. The timeline writes his name Yan-C Bin, without the second hyphen. In 970 a smaller victory of his leaked magic into the world and thickened the Evershroud fog. In 975 he took Carter. In the summer of 980 an agent of his died near Azure Lagoon and spoke the name.

### Lord Kaarak

Lord of Shakharra. The sheet gives the name as kay-rack. He holds the last key to Carter's underwater laboratory. His questions for McMasterson are under the Plane of Air. His NPC sheet is blank.

### Mister

Met in the fog at Evershroud. He gave Kayren the scarf. No portrait beyond that.

### Mara

Keeps the Blind Gull in Evershroud. Rooms are 1 sp a night. Fish and cheap ale.

### Father Samuel

**GM.** Priest at Evershroud. He is sure the fog has a greater cause, and he is set on lifting it. He has a triangulated source. Too many days, the fog pours from deep in the island, and it never reaches the mainland, so the source is on the island.

### Alistaire and Malistaire

They sit with Winston during Party Night and say they have kept him alive. They take the company to the Lady when asked, and they linger in her praise. No other description.

### The Lady of the House

An arch-fey, and the power at the Evershroud node. She led the Aetherian research there until the Enigma Project took her. The Enigma Accident hit her with enough magic to tear most people loose, and her will held. She is half-attached to her soul. Around the year 700 she came back to the house by that node. The fog is what the fae do to that node. It thickened in 970, and breathing it causes Mad Mist. Carter visited, was never charmed by a night in the house, went through the node, and never returned. She wishes he had stayed.

Alistaire and Malistaire keep her door and sit with guests the mist takes. In the summer of 980 the company finds her. The beast on the node just north of the manor is the Auran golem, crafted by the Aarakocra from Carter Wint's old notes. The company destroys it. She will be in their debt for that. Her rooms, and Carter Wint's visit, are under Evershroud. Her own sheet is blank.


### General Ignar Voryn

**GM.** Commander of the Flameguard at Bloomridge, in the Savagebloom Expanse. The handout is titled General Ignar. Over six feet, amber eyes, enchanted flame-resistant armor, and the greatsword Blazebane. He is involved with Aeris Thorne. The full portrait is under Bloomridge.

### Aeris Thorne

**GM.** Head scribe of Bloomridge. Young-looking half-elf, magical glasses, long black hair, a tall crumpled wizard hat. Her tent is books, papers, and stone from lost temples. The Goblin Map sends a searcher to the camp's scribes for the local ruins.

### Garrick "Firebeard" Stonefist

### Gorc

### Thaddeus Moorwind

Old human man. Wizard. He spent his life studying magic and arcane objects, and he never took a wife. He owns Moorwind Magics in Anchorfall, next door to The Staggering Steer. He adopted Taurus because he wanted to create something greater than a magic item or a magical apprentice. He owes the From Brews to Bullion party a debt for saving his son in the wine disaster. He pays it with his most prized object, a thing of fables he was saving until his son grew a sense of adventure. Taurus never did. It is the Goblin Map, the same clue Rex Refund and Scorpion Jack carried. He says, with a smirk, that the first step is translating it. He will also sell the party magical objects at a discount. The discount is not numbered.

### Taurus Moorwind

Adult minotaur man. Adopted son of Thaddeus. He owns The Staggering Steer and lives there with Gretchen Moorwind, his old black cat. He is in a dedicated relationship with Enoki Chanterelle, who works under him. He had a taste of combat during the wine disaster. It was not his cup of tea. Ale was. He is happy at home, brewing his own drink, and he is terrified of Enoki's coming adventure. The tavern and his father's shop now connect through the basement.

### Enoki Chanterelle
