#### Myconid Colony

Deep under the mountains, a sealed country with very few entrances. Things here grew without sunlight and without much to eat. Flora dominates: glowing mushrooms, mystic plants, strange fungi. A handful of dangerous fauna dominate their own ground. Legends talk about long-lost dwarves, and about a species of especially psychedelic mushroom.

The tribe, as the sheet lists them: Elder Mycelor, the Shroomcaller, Moldmangler, Sporebreaker, Sporecap, Mossfoot, Fungulus, Mycelius, Moldspore, Mosswhisper.

A false prophet has risen in the darkest corners. Kyuss, pronounced Kee-OOS, the Worm that Walks. He takes the bodies of enemies and of those who give themselves, by a parasitic infection. The worms get under the flesh and drive the host. The myconids thought they were safe. Their bodies lack the usual places for the worms, and they could reject them. Kyuss grew stronger. The worms now attack myconid flesh directly, tearing them apart alive and driving the infected mad, craving only death.

He is deeper in the dark, and his forces are spread through the Underdark. The tribe knows an object of power, deeper even than where Kyuss lies. He knows of it too. His spawn fill the dark that has to be waded to reach it. They ask the explorers to help retrieve it, and offer whatever help they can give in return. The object is not named on this sheet. Sporosylvan, in Khan's gear, is a rare Underdark fungus with a healing radius, and it is the only object these sheets describe that way.

#### Altar of Rot

Waist-deep murky gray water. A few floating patches of purple fungus light the room. In the center, a statue of a robed humanoid with no face. The body looks composed of long strands gathered into limbs. It holds a stone dish with an extremely shallow layer of dirt. East, a large arch and closed stone doors. Something is written over the arch in a strange runic script.

In Undercommon, the outside reads as a warning to return the way you came. It also reads Altar of Rot.

Anything set in the dish rots fast, becomes dirt, and filters into the statue. Anything living that touches the plate takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. Enough dirt and the hands lower, a single Worm of Kyuss shoots out, and the doors open.

Scratched on the wall, almost looking like natural stone, in Thieves' Cant: "Starving, durt hurts, stupid worms." The spelling "durt" is the sheet's.

Under the scratches, at the bottom of the water, a partly decomposed halfling. His pack has thieves' tools, a couple of small gems at 10 gp, a dozen old unidentifiable silver coins, and a dagger. An Investigation check finds a pair of Boots of Elvenkind buried in the muck. Those boots are later filed under Khan.
