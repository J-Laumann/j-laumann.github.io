## Flying Boats gear

Kincaid is said to obtain an Aurelic Crystal.

### Kayren

### Mister's Spider Scarf

Wondrous item, uncommon, attunement. Invisible spider silk. It sticks, and Mister did a good job of it. A creature that moves within 15 feet sends a vibration through the web, which is blindsight 15 feet.

**Cocoon.** When hit, a reaction wraps the wearer in webbing and turns them invisible until the start of their next turn. Three uses, recharging at dawn.

**Web.** Cast web once a day without a slot or materials. A caster always has it prepared.

**GM.** Cursed. A magical sweet scent draws bugs to the wearer, and any insectoid monster targets them.

### Philter of Love

Potion, uncommon. The next creature seen within 10 minutes becomes a charm for 1 hour. If it is a species and gender the drinker is normally attracted to, the drinker regards it as their true love for that hour. Rose-hued, effervescent, with one easy-to-miss heart-shaped bubble.

### Safety Bear

Wondrous item, uncommon. Held during a short rest, the holder regains an extra 3d6 hit points. Advantage on saves against being frightened.

### Candle of Borrowed Shadows

Wondrous item, uncommon. It sits on a silver platter and burns dimmer than a normal candle. Low light to 20 feet, and it does not burn down on its own. If mentally allowed, the light borrows the holder's shadow. The shadow breaks off, takes mental commands for 1 minute, can manipulate what it touches, and can carry up to 10 pounds. Five uses. The candle melts fast while the shadow is out, and the last charge reduces it to nothing.

### Bark of Debarking

Wondrous item, uncommon. Hold this mossy bark, touch a Large or larger inanimate plant, and use an action to mimic transport via plants. The destination is forced: the animated oak in front of Evershroud Manor.

### Flute of the Fae

Wondrous item, rare, attunement. Spells cast with it have +1 to spell attacks and to save DCs. If the spell charms on a failed save, that save is at disadvantage. While attuned, these spells are on the user's list and count as known, and each can be cast once a day without a slot: healing word at 3rd level, dispel magic, faerie fire, and protection from evil and good.

**GM.** A creature that tries to play it without attuning must pass a DC 15 Wisdom save or take 2d4 psychic damage.

### Pocket Cleric

Wondrous item, uncommon. A small silver figurine of a tiny cleric. Four charges, all returning at dawn.

One charge and a bonus action: touch a weapon and mimic bless with that weapon until the end of the user's next turn.

A number of charges and a bonus action: touch a firearm and make its next attack a blessed bullet. Shells cost 1 charge, medium 2, long 3.

Three charges: cast prayer of healing without a slot or components.

### Kincaid

### Floab Lance

Weapon, lance. 1d12 piercing, 3 pounds. Reach, special, thrown 20/60. It returns to the hand on a thrown miss. +1 to attack rolls. On a critical hit, an extra 1d6 thunder as it cuts the air. Disadvantage when attacking a target within 5 feet. Two hands when the wielder is not mounted.

### Powder Horn of Pazazz

Wondrous item, rare. Silver, carved with flowers and fairies, full of glittery dust. A bonus action pours it onto a piece of ammunition or a melee weapon. The next attack with that object, within 1 hour, is infused. Roll a d6. Three uses, recharging at dawn.

1. The weapon kicks out of the hand and drops 10 feet behind, and the attack deals double damage.
2. The weapon goes ethereal for a moment. The attack is mimed with the hands and deals psychic damage.
3. Force guides the weapon or the ammunition. The attack hits.
4. On a hit, minds link. The attacker learns what the target is thinking, as detect thoughts.
5. The attack becomes a bolt of wild energy, as lightning bolt.
6. The attack hits every enemy's weapons instead of dealing damage, and those weapons fly 10 feet behind them.

**GM.** On a misfire, the barrel explodes into pixie dust and mimics confusion centered on the shooter, DC 15.

### Pixie Scissors

Wondrous item, common. Golden scissors that treat magic thread and magic fabric as if they were ordinary.

### Spell Sewing Needle

Wondrous item, uncommon. Prick it against the skin and use it for the somatic component. It sews a floating thread of blood through the gestures. The spell is cast as if the slot were 2 levels higher, and the slot cannot go above 9th. The needle then becomes dust.

### Pipe of Blowing Smoke

Wondrous item, uncommon. While smoking it, lies sound magically convincing. Insight checks to catch the deception are at disadvantage. An action blows hard into the pipe and releases a cloud as fog cloud, centered on the smoker, who then coughs for 1 minute. Once, and again at dawn.

**GM.** Curse. If the lie is boring, or if it is not flattering, the pipe burns the throat. The smoker coughs for 1 minute and takes 2d6 psychic damage.

### Bangles of Auran Armory

Wondrous item, rare, attunement. A pair of silver bangles with glowing yellow lines. Either one hums if held to the ear. As an item interaction, or when rolling initiative, shroud a hand and part of the lower arm in Auran energy and make one weapon from the list. A two-handed weapon requires the other hand to merge in as well. The energy cannot leave the hand. Dismissing it takes no action. The weapon has +1 to attack and damage. When a damage die rolls its highest value, the additional effect can be inflicted. The form wavers. The sheet says it may be in disrepair.

| Weapon | Damage | Properties | Additional effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flail | 1d8 bludgeoning | — | Disadvantage on the target's next save before the end of its next turn |
| Battleaxe | 1d8 slashing | Versatile (1d10) | −15 feet of speed until the end of the target's next turn |
| Tentacle | 1d6 radiant | Reach | Grappled, escape DC 14 |
| Lance | 1d12 piercing | Reach, special | Disarmed |

The lance has disadvantage within 5 feet, and it takes two hands when the wielder is not mounted.

### Borin

### Pearlob

The whole player text is "Yummy!" and that Borin Pyre discovered it. Edith's pie is the only other use of the name.

### (Floab/Flobe) Ore

Things made of it are always under the effect of feather fall, and they weigh half as much as normal.

Armor of it does not impose disadvantage on Stealth, grants advantage on Acrobatics, and a reaction can cancel falling prone.

Weapons of it have double thrown range, or 20/60 if the weapon has no thrown range, and they return on a miss. They have +1 to hit and not to damage. On a critical hit, an extra 1d6 thunder as the weapon cuts the air.

**GM.** The true name is Aureleonite, given by the aarakocra who mine and work it. An older note spelled that Aurelionite. The party that found the ore apart from them called it Floab, a joke, and only they use that name. The sheet also writes Flobe.

### Gauntlets of Unyielding Grasp

Wondrous item, rare, attunement. Strength becomes 19. A Strength already 19 or higher does not change. A thrown weapon attack returns the weapon to the hand immediately.

### Pearl of Power

Wondrous item, uncommon, attunement by a spellcaster. An action and the command word regain one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower. Once, until the next dawn.

### +1 Floab Greatsword

Weapon, uncommon. +1 to attack and damage. On a critical hit, an extra 1d6 thunder. Thrown 20/60, and it returns on a miss.

### Winston

### Amulet of Last Whispers

Wondrous item, rare, attunement. Tarnished silver, an ivory skull set in an onyx pendant. Held to the ear, it whispers. While worn, otherworldly whispers come from any undead within 100 feet.

**Speak with dead.** Once a day, at dawn, through the amulet, without a slot or materials. The spell is always prepared.

**Last stand.** On the wearer's turn, while unconscious at 0 hit points, a burst of necromantic energy brings them to their feet with eyes open. They take that turn as normal and cannot gain hit points while in the state. At the end of the turn they fall unconscious again and make a death save as normal. Twice before a long rest, or three times ever, and the energy takes the body. The wearer becomes a mindless zombie instead of dropping.

**GM.** Curse. Late at night, or where the veil is thin, the dead may speak with the living and put questions to the wearer. If the wearer dies while attuned, they rise next turn as a zombie.

### Ointments

No bearer is named.

**Borrowed Beauty.** Think of a humanoid already seen, and apply it. The treated places change to match that person for 1 hour, voice included if the face is treated. Enemies have disadvantage on seeing through it.

**Slipping Form.** For 1 minute, advantage on checks to escape a grapple, and squeezing has none of its usual disadvantages.

**Balsam Balm.** Heals 2d8+4, and removes poisoned, blinded, frightened, or charmed.
