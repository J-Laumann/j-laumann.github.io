Magic items

The Sun Prism stayed with Tor Longtooth after he took it at the Sunfire collapse. The Orecrusher staff is with Wendy.

Potion of Heroism

Potion, rare. For 1 hour, 10 temporary hit points, and the effect of bless with no concentration. The blue potion bubbles and steams as if boiling.

Holy Nog

Potion, rare. Regain 8d4+8 hit points. Any effect of iceblight ends immediately, and for the next minute the drinker has resistance to cold.

Bronze Hero Badge

Wondrous item, artifact. A detached scale of Torren Blackholt, an ancient bronze dragon, made so the Heroes of Emberheim can be reached. He can put a mental charge through it, from a hum to a near-painful jolt. Set on a corpse for 6 seconds, it replicates revivify once, then never again. A divination on each badge tracks where it is. The number 151 is carved into the back. The same number is the first line of Team Rating (DMG).

Clint

Ring of Demonic Dominance

Wondrous item, very rare. The ring glows a dull crimson, and it whispers if held to the ear. An action to cast dominate monster, DC 18. Once until the next dawn.

GM. Curse. Once worn, it will not come off until Remove Curse. After every long rest, a DC 18 Wisdom save. On a failure, sometime the next day the ring takes the body, as dominate monster. The whispers are suggestions from the nine circles.

Ring of Y'ghnath'ull

The sheet's title is written in a corrupted, unreadable script. The readable letters resolve to Y'ghnath'ull. Wondrous item, very rare. Requires a warlock with Pact of the Blade.

Pact weapons from that feature gain an extra +1 to attack and damage. A bonus action creates a pact weapon in an empty hand. A ranged pact weapon cannot be loaded with ordinary ammunition. It can be summoned already loaded, three times, recharging at dawn. Curse: the ring stays on until Remove Curse is cast on the wearer. The GM side of the sheet is empty.

Ring of Spell Storing

Ring, rare, attunement. Holds up to 5 levels of spells. When found, it contains 1d6−1 levels of spells chosen by the DM. Any creature can cast a spell of 1st through 5th into the ring by touching it as the spell is cast. The spell is stored instead of taking effect. If it does not fit, it is spent for nothing. The slot used is the space used. The wearer casts a stored spell with the original caster's slot, save DC, attack bonus, and ability. It then leaves the ring.

Wand of Secrets

Wand, and an arcane focus. Uncommon. 3 charges. An action and 1 charge: if a secret door or a trap is within 30 feet, the wand pulses and points at the nearest. It regains 1d3 charges at dawn. Weight 1.

Rod of the Pact Keeper (+1)

Rod, uncommon. While held, a bonus to warlock spell attacks and to the save DCs of warlock spells. The sheet says the bonus is set by the rod's rarity. The title gives the +1. An action regains one warlock spell slot. Not again until a long rest.

Mind Sharpener

A suit of armor or robes. 4 charges. When the wearer fails the Constitution save to keep concentration, they can use a reaction and 1 charge to succeed instead. Regains 1d4 charges at dawn.

Soul Orb

Wondrous item, legendary. Used to bind a soul. While holding it, cast banishment, DC 18. A fiend or celestial has disadvantage. On a failure, soul and body are trapped in the orb and can be seen inside it. If concentration is held the full minute, the soul stays, whatever plane it came from. The caster cannot choose to drop concentration on a banishment cast this way. The orb holds one soul. It must be shattered to release the one inside. Found in the right arm of the Shattered Colossus.

Robe of Useful Items

Wondrous item, uncommon. Cloth patches. An action detaches one, and it becomes the thing it shows. The last patch leaves an ordinary garment. A proper merchant can add 1d4 random patches for 100 gp. Patches with a cash value are rerolled.

Patches already on it: two daggers, two bullseye lanterns filled and lit, two steel mirrors, two 10-foot poles, two 50-foot coils of hempen rope, two sacks, three potions of healing marked (4), an iron door, four portable rams, four spell scrolls, two windows, a rowboat, two 24-foot ladders. The sheet also says the robe has 4d4 other patches, DM's choice or the table below. Several of those results repeat patches already listed. Both lists are the sheet's.

Animated Shield

Armor, shield, very rare, attunement. Speak the command word as a bonus action. The shield leaps up and hovers in your space, protecting you as if wielded, hands free. It stays animated for 1 minute, until you end it as a bonus action, or until you are incapacitated or dead. Then it falls, or drops into a free hand. A shield is worth +2 AC, and only one shield benefits you. Weight 6.

Luckstone

Wondrous item, uncommon, attunement. On your person, +1 to ability checks and saving throws.

Holy Symbol of Darkstone

Wondrous item, legendary. Attunement by a non-evil character. A platinum sun with a large topaz at the center. Older than the cross, and older than what the sheet calls the second coming. Legend: an angel in the form of a giant bird brought it to a paladin in need. He used it to root out vampire nests until he died, and Darkstone had its golden age. The kings wore it after him. Krieg was keeping it in the wax of his own chest.

10 charges. Regains 1d6+4 at dawn.

Hold vampires. 1 charge, an action. Present it. Vampires and vampire spawn within 30 feet, DC 15 Wisdom or paralyzed for 1 minute. Repeat at the end of each turn.

Turn undead. If you have Turn Undead or Turn the Unholy, spend 3 charges when you present the symbol with that feature. Undead have disadvantage on the save.

Sunlight. 5 charges, an action. Bright light 30 feet, dim light 30 feet beyond. It is sunlight. 10 minutes, or until you end it, no action required.

Augury. An action, no material component. Once until dawn, or refresh it by spending 1 charge.

Cure wounds. 2 charges, an action. One creature you can see within 30 feet regains 3d8+3 hit points, unless it is undead, a construct, or a fiend.

Divine intervention. An action. Present the symbol, call out to God, and cast wish. The symbol then crumbles to dust.

Skull of Stefan Gregorovich

Stefan was the first counselor of King Krieg von Darkstone. The skull animates when spoken to and answers five questions as if speak with dead had been cast. Recharges at dawn. GM. He was neither observant nor well informed. His answers are usually untrue.

Skull of Intree Sik-Valoo

A sage of ancient Darkstone. The same five-question speak-with-dead skull, recharging at dawn. GM. He was educated and astute. Most of his answers are true, as far as he knew.

Sanctum Amulet

Wondrous item, very rare, attunement. A black opal on a pearlescent chain. The sacred rune is on the back of the pendant. Resistance to necrotic damage. Spare the Dying as an action or a bonus action.

Invoking the rune. When a creature you can see within 60 feet would drop to 0 hit points from damage, a reaction makes the pendant flash pale. A pale white aura covers the creature, and it ignores the damage that would have dropped it. Once until the next dawn.

+1 Crystal

Adventuring gear, arcane focus, uncommon. Spells cast through it have +1 to spell attack rolls and to saving throws. Over a long rest it can be infused into an item, carrying these abilities onto that object. Reversed over another long rest.

Amulet of Shifting Seaweed

Wondrous item, uncommon, attunement. Once per long rest, cast polymorph on yourself. The beast you become looks made entirely of wet, living seaweed. Treasure of the kelpie in Sunfire Temple, area 10.

Revenue Rifle

Weapon, Henry lever, uncommon, attunement. +1 to attack and damage. When you roll a 20 against a Small or larger hostile creature, you magically earn gold pieces equal to the attack's damage. The barrel glimmers like a coin, and the amount bursts out of the wound.

Staff of the Cauldron Carrier

Staff, uncommon, attunement. Iron and brass, a cauldron at the end, and only as heavy as a normal staff. +1 on ability checks with alchemy supplies, cook's utensils, or an herbalism kit. 3 charges, all restored at dawn. An action spends charges to cast, with your save DC and spell attack: burning hands 1, create food and water 3, detect poison and disease 1, heat metal 2, purify food and drink 1. This create food and water grants 1d6+4 temporary hit points to anyone who eats a meal it makes. The food is delicious. The Orecrusher vault's right-hand display case is this staff: brass bulb, iron scepter, hooked brass head, small brass cauldron.

Marksman's Cloak

Wondrous item, rare. Attunement by a ranger. You can concentrate on a second ranger spell at the same time as hunter's mark. A Constitution save to keep concentration is one save for both. Fail, and both drop. If hunter's mark is the only one, damage cannot break it.

Phantomstrike Rifle

Weapon, bolt-action rifle, very rare, attunement. Special ammunition, 60 gp for 3. 1d12 piercing, 12 pounds, range 50/200. Two-handed, reload 3, misfire 0, telescopic sight. +2 to attack and damage. Instead of a normal bullet, you may fire a Phantom Round: blue ethereal energy, a faint spectral wail instead of a blast, soaking into the first creature hit. The wail is heard only within 50 feet. The shot ignores total cover and passes walls, if you know where the target is. Three Phantom Rounds a day, at dawn. Killing a creature with the rifle also restores one.

Boots of Elvenkind

Wondrous item, uncommon. Your steps make no sound, on any surface. Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently. The pair under the water at the Altar of Rot is this item.

Sporosylvan

Wondrous item, artifact. A small fungus known only deep in the Underdark. Soft pulsing green light. Pressed to exposed skin, it attaches and puts down shallow roots. A fresh one has charges equal to the user's level. An action spends a charge: it cures diseases and removes curses on every creature within 10 feet, in a soothing aura of spores. The sheet's verb is "curse diseases," which reads as a slip for cure. Emptied, it withers until you gain a level, and then it regains 1 charge. This is the only named Underdark object of power on these sheets. The myconids' unnamed relic is not identified with it.

Buckler of Bone

Shield, uncommon, attunement. It attaches to the user's soul and sits on the forearm by magnetism, leaving the hand free. Up to 3 charges. A charge returns when a creature of significant threat, the DM's call, dies within 10 feet. You always have false life prepared, and you may spend a charge to cast it as a bonus action without a slot.

The Pretty Pistol

Weapon, uncommon. While this weapon is visible on your person, +2 to Persuasion.

Goggles of Night

Wondrous item, eyes, uncommon. The sheet spells it "uncomon." Darkvision 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, the range increases by 60 feet. Also in the treasure of Sunfire area 17.

Delectable Dice

Wondrous item, uncommon. A bonus action eats one candy die and grants 1 lucky point, as the Lucky feat. Points gained this way vanish on a long rest.

Coin of Divination

Wondrous item, uncommon. A small silver coin. Lucky Luke's smirking face on one side, a divination symbol on the other. 1 charge of Portent. At dawn it regains the charge and rerolls the die. While you have the coin, you may activate it and use that Portent die as the feature is normally used.

Eyes of the Eagle

Wondrous item, uncommon, attunement. Crystal lenses. Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight. In clear visibility, you can make out details of creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across, at an extreme distance.

Infernal Fang Dagger

Weapon, dagger, rare. +1 to attack and damage. An extra 1d6 fire on a hit, or 2d6 if the target is a celestial. Once per long rest, cast vampiric touch through the dagger. Found in the right arm of the Shattered Colossus.

Luck Blade

Weapon, any sword, legendary, attunement. +1 to attack and damage. While the sword is on your person, +1 to saving throws. Luck: no action, reroll one attack, check, or save you dislike. You must take the second roll. Once until dawn. The sheet stops there. It does not list a wish, even though many luck blades have one.

Heaven's Lance

Weapon, nitro rifle, very rare, attunement. +2 to attack and damage. Radiant instead of piercing. Once on each of your turns, you may add 2d6 radiant on a hit. In open air, an action fires both cylinders into the clouds, no ammunition spent, and tears a rift. A beam centered on a point within normal range: a cylinder 10 feet in radius and 60 feet tall. DC 18 Dexterity save, 6d10 fire and 6d10 radiant, half on a success. Once until dawn. GM. The sheet prices it at 12,000 gold.

Henry Stormshot

Firearm, Henry lever, rare. A polished wooden stock, silver along the barrel, blue arcane energy in burn-marks shaped like a lightning bolt. 1d8 lightning instead of piercing. Critical hits triple all dice instead of doubling them. Once per long rest, an action replicates lightning bolt, DC 16, by firing the rifle.

Shard of Warmth

Wondrous item, uncommon. While holding this golden crystal, resistance to cold. It also sheds physical warmth, 10 feet of bright light, and 10 feet of dim light. Maximum 3 charges. It loses 1 charge each dawn. At 0 charges it does nothing until it has at least 1 again. Anyone may speak a warm memory into it. That grants 1 or more charges, by how warm the memory is, and the speaker loses the memory permanently. This is the same trade the frost giants of the Ninth Circle ask for out loud.

Bottle of Boundless Coffee

Wondrous item, common. A metal bottle of delicious warm coffee. The stopper is chained to the bottle. Even open, it refuses any liquid but the coffee it makes. The coffee stays comfortably warm, and the heat cannot be felt through the metal. GM. Each time you drink, roll a d20. On a 1, it refuses to pour for the next hour. Coffee poured out, rather than drunk from the bottle, vanishes the moment it leaves.

Hunter's Best Buddy

Weapon, dagger, uncommon. +1 to attack and damage. While wielding it, +2 to Survival checks and +1 to carving rolls.

Double Jumpers

Wondrous item, uncommon. Leather and brass boots. A bonus action fires an explosion from the soles. Every creature within 5 feet, including you, makes a DC 15 Strength save. Failure: 2d8 thunder and pushed 10 feet away from you. Success: half damage, not pushed. You may choose to fail. If you fail, you are pushed 15 feet in a direction you choose instead of 10. Once, recharging on a short or long rest.

Hunter's Coat

Armor, leather, very rare, attunement. +2 AC. 3 charges. When you hit a creature that is not at full hit points, spend 1 charge to deal an extra 1d10 necrotic. Regains 1d3 charges at dawn.

Lantern of Truth

Wondrous item, rare. A hooded lantern. 6 hours on 1 pint of oil. Bright light 20 feet, dim light 20 feet beyond. A second funnel takes a pint of "Ethereal Oil." The light then animates, and anything within 20 feet is revealed as if by truesight, for 1 hour. An action lowers the hood, reducing the light to dim light in 5 feet.

Scale-shot

Weapon, Terminus shotgun, uncommon. +1 to attack and damage. Every shot also puffs beautifully colored kulu-ya-ku feathers out of the barrel.

The Opportune Uppercut

Firearm, uppercut, uncommon. If anyone but the "owner" tries to fire it, the attack is always a misfire. The owner changes only if the current owner willingly and magically gives it a new owner, or if someone of high enough magical intellect re-enchants a new owner onto it. When the owner spends a lucky point to reroll an attack with it, they may reroll normally, or they may choose any result of 10 or under.

The Lucky Lid

Wondrous item, a hat, uncommon. Once per long rest, when you are targeted by an attack that would be a critical hit, a reaction turns that attack into a miss.

Chains of Ethereal Torment

Wondrous item, a shackle, rare, attunement. Essence of the Ethereal Plane. 6 charges, regaining 1d6 at dawn. Spellcasting, DC 15: shape water 1, mirror image 2, hold person 2, fear 3, banishment 5.

Chilled to the bone. Resistance to cold. If something else already grants resistance, this becomes immunity. If something else already grants immunity, you also heal for half of any cold damage you would have taken.

Cursed. Once attuned, the shackles are magically locked and cannot be taken off. Body heat falls to 40 degrees Fahrenheit. The shackles rattle in the dark and impose disadvantage on Stealth. Remove Curse unlocks them and ends the attunement.

Her Handkerchief

Wondrous item, uncommon. While you have it, you always have absorb elements prepared, and you can cast it from the handkerchief with no components. Once until dawn. It still smells of apricots. It was behind the portrait in the audience hall at Castle Darkstone, with the poisoned wine and the 500 gp diamond ring.

Axe Beak Tomahawk

Weapon, handaxe, uncommon. Made from a slain axe beak. The first time on each of your turns that you hit a creature with a flying speed, that creature makes a DC 13 Strength save. Each failure cuts its flying speed by 15 feet for 1 minute. Bloomridge's bounty is the trade that wants axe-beak parts for axes.

Frostweaver Gloves

Wondrous item, gloves, rare, attunement. Dark leather, unnaturally cold. Small icicles form on the fingertips overnight. Unarmed strikes deal an extra 1d4 cold. 3 charges, all restored at dawn. An action and 1 charge: a ball of frigid energy, molded into either sleet storm or ice storm, DC 15. Sleet storm costs you 4d6 cold. Ice storm costs you 8d6 cold. Either way, your hands numb, and your next attack roll has disadvantage. You must then throw the ball at a point in the spell's range. Found on the clothes in the frozen chest at the Pine ranch.

Urnblade

Weapon, scimitar, rare, attunement. It looks like a small cremation urn. A bonus action and the first command word unseals the lid. A curved blade of dark smoke pours out. The word again reseals it and the blade vanishes. Proficiency with shortswords, scimitars, or longswords covers the urnblade. +1 to attack and damage. Necrotic instead of slashing.

Shadow ally. An action and the second command word. Your shadow detaches and becomes a shadow in an empty space you can see within 10 feet. You command it mentally on your turn if you are not incapacitated: a specific action and a move, or a general order such as attack or guard. The DM has its statistics. This shadow does not spawn another when a non-evil humanoid dies to it. While the shadow exists, the weapon's bonus becomes +2. If you are attacked and the shadow is within 5 feet, a reaction makes the shadow the target instead.

The shadow vanishes at 0 hit points, beyond 120 feet, or when you dismiss it as an action. Your cast shadow returns. If it dropped to 0, there is a cumulative 5 percent chance the urn turns to dust and is destroyed. Qesnef, in Sunfire area 27, wears one.

Weapon, longsword, legendary, attunement. A +2 longsword.

Master of metal. An action. Take control of any humanoid wearing metal armor, as dominate person. 1 minute. Once per long rest.

Arcane armor. An action. Throw your consciousness into a visible, unworn suit of metal armor until you dispel it, no action required, or until the armor is destroyed. Steamroll. When you take the Dash action, a bonus action makes one melee weapon attack or a shove. If you moved at least 15 feet in a straight line first, you either add +5 to the damage of that hit, or you push the shoved target up to 10 feet.

Barroth Ridge Armor

Armor, scale mail, uncommon. Same shared-attunement rule as the helm. AC 14 plus Dexterity, maximum 2, as scale mail.

Mud slinger. As a reaction to being targeted by an attack, the armor wraps you in enchanted mud: +5 AC until the start of your next turn. Once, then a short or long rest. Dusthaven pays 20 gp for proof of a barroth. These two pieces are the only barroth crafts written up.

Ring of Seething

Ring, uncommon, attunement. A dark band, cool, cracked across an otherwise smooth face. When you attune, choose aberration, celestial, dragon, elemental, fey, fiend, or undead. Within 60 feet of that type, the band glows red hot. You have advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened by that type. It feels only faintly warm to you. Small pieces of the band hover off the surface while it glows.

Curse. Once it is on the finger, Remove Curse is required to take it off. While cursed, disadvantage on Charisma checks and on Wisdom (Insight) checks involving the chosen type. Within 60 feet of one or more of them, disadvantage on attack rolls against a creature of any other type. The cracked silver ring in the Orecrusher vault's left chest matches this description.

Scorpion Lasher

Weapon, whip, uncommon, attunement. Built from salvaged pieces of a giant scorpion. It drips a sickly venom. Once on each of your turns when you hit, you can add 1d6 poison. If the creature is Medium or smaller, it also makes a DC 13 Strength save or is pulled 5 feet closer. The Dusthaven hive core near Scorpion Gulch is one of these, stuck in the floor by the handle.

The Sun Prism

Wondrous item, artifact. Attunement by a spellcaster. A flawless many-faced crystal with the sun in it. Beams move in the core. Warmth and light in every direction.

Solar flare. An action. A blinding burst in 30 feet. Undead and fiends, DC 18 Constitution. Failure: 6d10 radiant and blinded for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each turn. Success: half damage, not blinded. Once until dawn.

Healing radiance. Held aloft, a command word. Gentle healing light. Every creature in 20 feet regains 4d8+8 hit points. Once until dawn.

Daylight. An action. Daylight centered on the prism, at will, lasting as long as you concentrate.

You always have guiding bolt prepared, and it does not count against prepared spells. You may also cast it at 1st level without a slot. Resistance to radiant damage. Advantage on saves against blindness.

How it is made, and who wants it, is the Aetheria chapter. Elowen's journal calls the same working the Sunfire Prism.

Wondrous item, uncommon. While wearing it, you may try to hide in plain sight in dim light or darkness. In darkness, you may cast invisibility without a slot. Once per day, recharging at noon. Emilio is not otherwise mentioned.

Ring of Animal Influence

Heart Seeker Bullet

Ammunition, bullet, uncommon. The tip pulses a dull red and pulls toward a weak point. Weapon attacks with this round crit on a 19 or a 20. Misfire increases by 1.

Flak Shell

Ammunition, shotgun shell, uncommon. On a hit, normal damage, then a Strength save, DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier plus your proficiency bonus. Failure: the target falls prone, and a fly speed is removed. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. Misfire increases by 1.

Lightning Rod Bullet

Ammunition, bullet, uncommon. The back of the round expands into a collapsing copper rod and sticks in the target. After a hit, weapon and spell attacks that deal lightning have advantage against that target, and the target has disadvantage on saves against lightning damage, until it uses an attack or an action to pull the rod out. Misfire increases by 1.

Blessed Bullets

Shell or compact. An attack with this round deals no damage. A creature other than you that is hit regains hit points equal to 2d4 plus your Dexterity and Wisdom modifiers, minimum 1. Advantage on the attack if the target is friendly. The round dissolves into healing magic and is destroyed. Undead take that amount as radiant damage instead. Constructs are unaffected.

Medium uses 4d4. Long uses 8d4.

Sold at both churches. Shells 30 gp, medium 150 gp, long 1,500 gp.

Bullet of Anchoring

Weapon, bullet, uncommon. This piece of steel weighs 1 pound and otherwise looks and flies like a normal bullet. On a hit, a chain flies from the wound into the nearest floor or wall. The creature makes a DC 13 Strength save. Failure: grappled by the chain for 1 minute, the chain lengthening and wrapping. Success: speed is halved until the start of your next turn. A grappled creature can use an action to repeat the save and pull free. When the effect ends, the round becomes a 2-foot length of nonmagical chain.

Permafrost Pellets

GM. Ammunition, shell, rare. Freezing to the touch, and apparently made of magic ice. The attack deals cold instead of its usual type, plus 1d6 cold. The target makes a DC 14 Constitution save or its speed is halved for 1 minute. Repeat at the end of each turn. A pouch of 2d6 of these is up the chimney at the Pine ranch, with the knuckle-shotgun blueprints.

Philter of Love

Potion, uncommon. The next creature seen within 10 minutes becomes a charm for 1 hour. If it is a species and gender the drinker is normally attracted to, the drinker regards it as their true love for that hour. Rose-hued, effervescent, with one easy-to-miss heart-shaped bubble.

Safety Bear

Wondrous item, uncommon. Held during a short rest, the holder regains an extra 3d6 hit points. Advantage on saves against being frightened.

Candle of Borrowed Shadows

Wondrous item, uncommon. It sits on a silver platter and burns dimmer than a normal candle. Low light to 20 feet, and it does not burn down on its own. If mentally allowed, the light borrows the holder's shadow. The shadow breaks off, takes mental commands for 1 minute, can manipulate what it touches, and can carry up to 10 pounds. Five uses. The candle melts fast while the shadow is out, and the last charge reduces it to nothing.

Bark of Debarking

Wondrous item, uncommon. Hold this mossy bark, touch a Large or larger inanimate plant, and use an action to mimic transport via plants. The destination is forced: the animated oak in front of Evershroud Manor.

Flute of the Fae

Wondrous item, rare, attunement. Spells cast with it have +1 to spell attacks and to save DCs. If the spell charms on a failed save, that save is at disadvantage. While attuned, these spells are on the user's list and count as known, and each can be cast once a day without a slot: healing word at 3rd level, dispel magic, faerie fire, and protection from evil and good.

GM. A creature that tries to play it without attuning must pass a DC 15 Wisdom save or take 2d4 psychic damage.

Pocket Cleric

Wondrous item, uncommon. A small silver figurine of a tiny cleric. Four charges, all returning at dawn.

One charge and a bonus action: touch a weapon and mimic bless with that weapon until the end of the user's next turn.

A number of charges and a bonus action: touch a firearm and make its next attack a blessed bullet. Shells cost 1 charge, medium 2, long 3.

Three charges: cast prayer of healing without a slot or components.

Weapon, lance. 1d12 piercing, 3 pounds. Reach, special, thrown 20/60. It returns to the hand on a thrown miss. +1 to attack rolls. On a critical hit, an extra 1d6 thunder as it cuts the air. Disadvantage when attacking a target within 5 feet. Two hands when the wielder is not mounted.

Powder Horn of Pazazz

Wondrous item, rare. Silver, carved with flowers and fairies, full of glittery dust. A bonus action pours it onto a piece of ammunition or a melee weapon. The next attack with that object, within 1 hour, is infused. Roll a d6. Three uses, recharging at dawn.

1. The weapon kicks out of the hand and drops 10 feet behind, and the attack deals double damage.

2. The weapon goes ethereal for a moment. The attack is mimed with the hands and deals psychic damage.

3. Force guides the weapon or the ammunition. The attack hits.

4. On a hit, minds link. The attacker learns what the target is thinking, as detect thoughts.

5. The attack becomes a bolt of wild energy, as lightning bolt.

6. The attack hits every enemy's weapons instead of dealing damage, and those weapons fly 10 feet behind them.

GM. On a misfire, the barrel explodes into pixie dust and mimics confusion centered on the shooter, DC 15.

Pixie Scissors

Wondrous item, common. Golden scissors that treat magic thread and magic fabric as if they were ordinary.

Spell Sewing Needle

Wondrous item, uncommon. Prick it against the skin and use it for the somatic component. It sews a floating thread of blood through the gestures. The spell is cast as if the slot were 2 levels higher, and the slot cannot go above 9th. The needle then becomes dust.

Pipe of Blowing Smoke

Wondrous item, uncommon. While smoking it, lies sound magically convincing. Insight checks to catch the deception are at disadvantage. An action blows hard into the pipe and releases a cloud as fog cloud, centered on the smoker, who then coughs for 1 minute. Once, and again at dawn.

GM. Curse. If the lie is boring, or if it is not flattering, the pipe burns the throat. The smoker coughs for 1 minute and takes 2d6 psychic damage.

(Floab/Flobe) Ore

Things made of it are always under the effect of feather fall, and they weigh half as much as normal.

Armor of it does not impose disadvantage on Stealth, grants advantage on Acrobatics, and a reaction can cancel falling prone.

Weapons of it have double thrown range, or 20/60 if the weapon has no thrown range, and they return on a miss. They have +1 to hit and not to damage. On a critical hit, an extra 1d6 thunder as the weapon cuts the air.

GM. The true name is Aureleonite, given by the aarakocra who mine and work it. An older note spelled that Aurelionite. The party that found the ore apart from them called it Floab, a joke, and only they use that name. The sheet also writes Flobe.

Amulet of Last Whispers

Wondrous item, rare, attunement. Tarnished silver, an ivory skull set in an onyx pendant. Held to the ear, it whispers. While worn, otherworldly whispers come from any undead within 100 feet.

Speak with dead. Once a day, at dawn, through the amulet, without a slot or materials. The spell is always prepared.

Last stand. On the wearer's turn, while unconscious at 0 hit points, a burst of necromantic energy brings them to their feet with eyes open. They take that turn as normal and cannot gain hit points while in the state. At the end of the turn they fall unconscious again and make a death save as normal. Twice before a long rest, or three times ever, and the energy takes the body. The wearer becomes a mindless zombie instead of dropping.

GM. Curse. Late at night, or where the veil is thin, the dead may speak with the living and put questions to the wearer. If the wearer dies while attuned, they rise next turn as a zombie.
