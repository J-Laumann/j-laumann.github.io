Campaigns

Weird West's party is the Heroes of Highrest. Lost City is the same year, a party of two who did not find the Lost City of Gold. Flying Boats is an expedition in 980. From Brews to Bullion is the current party, also in 980: Toshiro Haru, Enoki Chanterelle, Marvin Giurelli Jr., and Magnolia Oriane. It opens on the wine disaster at The Staggering Steer, and Thaddeus Moorwind pays the rescuers with a clue to the city those two never found.

Weird West

971. Highrest is founded that year.

The company that the fountain is cutting becomes the Heroes of Highrest. Wendy, Az, Khan, and Clint are the four human points of view. Gulp is the frog. BBQ is the construct. Tor Longtooth travels with them until he leaves.

They clear Castle Darkstone of Aric. Paul Marauder dies in the castle and is buried in the town that grows under the cliff. The company leads the Marauders there. The town dubs them the Heroes of Highrest. The square's statue is theirs.

Before that march, the Marauders are one gang. Zephyr Scorchblade and Sirocco Emberweaver lead them as desert raiders. Tor leaves the company and joins that band, and he pulls it together around a home for downcast people with dark pasts. Their last raid is Dusthaven: citizens held on the northeast edge, the demand being the town's information on the Sun Prism. The company stops it, kills Zephyr and Sirocco, frees the captives, and is given the information. Tor follows them in secret to the Sunfire Temple, takes the Sun Prism, and from then on they work with him because he forces it. He makes them lead north of Dusthaven to Castle Darkstone.

In Ironspire they are called to the palace, sit the Contest of Heroes, and are hired for the cannon theft, the cold sack, and the work a fixer will not explain. The audience ends with six councilors looking down. The request is not written down. Archbishop Evalyne's chair is empty.

Clive Orecrusher still lives in Scorpion Gulch when the Heroes of Highrest help him reclaim his family's hall. The helmet of identification from that hall is why he later keeps Crystals and Curios.

What that campaign never finishes is in Still open. The zaratan is still pointed at Highrest when Weird West stops.

The Stolen Schematic

Dr. Verid Threx hires the party. He is a meticulous, morally driven gnome artificer who builds defensive technology. His invention was a scalable arcane railgun for city defense: the Arcstrike Cannon, firing a concentrated mana beam able to cut steel at immense distance. His own named sheet, Dr. Verid Threx, is otherwise blank.

Meryn Bronzethumb is the rival: a brilliant, unscrupulous half-elf, fond of shortcuts, quick cheap weapons, and one-upping competitors. He stole the plans and scaled them into an automated artillery cannon that tracks targets with arcane sensors and an unstable power source.

The Rust Rats are the mercenary thieves Meryn hired. One sentence of the sheet also calls them the Rustcloaks. The name is the Rust Rats. They are known for efficiency and anonymity. Their leader is Jessa "Redwren" Skorr, a human rogue with mechanical limbs. They broke Threx's vault with a concoction of rust-monster antennae, ate the door, and delivered the plans.

Meryn's Arcstrike Turret is larger, rotates fully, and fires rapid blasts. The core can explode. At initiative 20, creatures make a DC 17 Dexterity save, 8d8 force on a failure, half on a success.

Underground Gigs

GM. Three jobs from a fixer who does not explain himself.

  • Art swap. A wealthy client wants a painting in the Museum of Magitech History exchanged for a perfect forgery. The piece is guarded. Pay is 600 gp.
  • Sabotage. Cut power to section B-5 from a control station, and make it look accidental. No questions. Pay is 250 gp.
  • Train robbery. A bullet train is coming in with barrels of liquid mana. Nobody is paying the fixer. Other parties are interested. He will help sell the take and expects a finder's fee.

Sack of Cold

GM. The sack leads to the Ninth Circle. The party was hired to find Saint Nicholas. They succeeded, brought back his corpse and others, and killed Velkhana, the current Guardian of Souls. Pay was 15 platinum each. They were offered a sale of their Velkhana crystal, one of two. Will settled on 250 gp a week of research. The crystal would be returned with the findings.

Nicholas revives and recovers. A week later he finds the party, thanks them, says he knew they could do it, and introduces himself to anyone new. He answers questions, presents what he actually found, and tells them to apply as heroes. Hiring "randos he knew" cost him a miserable amount of searching and paperwork.

The circle itself, Krampus's gate, and the Pine ranch storm are under The Ninth Circle and Dusthaven.

The Contest of Heroes

GM. Four trials, then a registrar, then a banquet, then a second test.

Test of Iron. A metal portcullis. The lever is on the far side.

Test of Reflex. A thin wooden bridge wobbles over a dark pit. Logs swing across it and vanish into wall slots, then swing again.

Test of Light. The hall ends in a dark room. Walls run out of sight left and right. A sudden light burns too bright to watch. A DC 16 Constitution save lets a character look. The room is a maze of simple traps, mostly pits, with an exit into the arena.

Test of Combat. The light dies. An open dusty ground, pools of water, stairs up to a stone bridge. A brass guardian on the bridge beckons, then steps back out of sight.

Past that, a fancy double door and a gray-haired man in a black suit and bow tie, bushy moustache. He welcomes them to the Contest of Heroes, takes names, asks if they are registering as a party, and opens the doors. One side he pulls. The other opens by itself.

The banquet hall has a few dozen round tables, candelabras, and the imperial banner. About half the tables are already taken.

Wendy sees a steel construct in BBQ's league, then two classmates from Iron Spire Academy. Lysander Malcove, an arrogant and undeniable half-elf genius, top of the class, a tinkerer, a student of chronurgy. Kellen Duskwright, who made up Lysander's martial weakness and took to runes. Contest of Heroes: Rivals puts Kellen behind Lysander, not far behind him in intellect, and the two of them have built a shield guardian. They want to be heroes to prove themselves and to lock in the money.

Az recognizes the Graves siblings from Dusthaven. Before the party existed they stole bounties out from under Az, then left for Steamwood years ago. Evelyn, Cyrus, and Gideon. Evelyn carries a rifle, Cyrus a pistol, and Gideon a shotgun. All three are exceptional shots.

Khan, long gone from the city, picks out old bottom-dwellers seated with strangers rather than with each other. One of them was the Robin Hood of those years: the Dagger of the People, blackmailer of corrupt nobles and killer of lawless judges. Almost nobody knows the face. Khan does. Sorrin Quill, a black-feathered aarakocra.

Clint is surprised to see local legends. Haruki Kurosawa claims another world, carries a legendary blade from home, and has the will to beat all evil. He is a hero in every way except the official one, and he has a fan club. Talia Moss, the Verdant Viper, is a half-elf druid. Green tattoos and serpent gear come from an upbringing in the Savagebloom Expanse. She is a vigilante, and the real law keeps catching up with her.

Torren Blackholt speaks from a podium: tall, broad-shouldered, black hair swept back, blackened steel plate, a massive shield across his back. He is a retired military leader and the new Leader of the Heroes of Emberheim. Food is meat, cheese, fruit, ale, and wine. He tells them they made it through the front door, that the later tests will be harder and eventually against each other, and that he wants several parties to leave as official heroes with nothing dirty between contests. The first test is back through the doors they entered, in an hour.

After a thinning of the field he welcomes the survivors, points at four butlers who bow together, and says each party will be walked out in an order set by their performance. Leaving first is a slight advantage.

4 Statues

The puzzle, as the players see it: A says "B is telling the truth." B says "D is lying." C says "I am the truthful one." D says "C is lying."

GM. One floating light, stone doors, four hulking stone warriors labeled A, B, C, and D. The inscription: "Three deceive, one speaks true. Choose the truth." Each statue has a pedestal and a button. The door shuts and the statues speak the lines above.

The wrong button wakes that statue. "Fooled you!" and a stone fist. The right button makes all four say "Good luck!" The center pair step aside. The doors open on a small room with four stone chairs, a rune in each back, and a slide. The carving asks that all combat equipment, constructs included, go down the slide. Anything living that takes the slide bets its life. Then sit.

The runes flash blue and hum. The sitter wakes in the arena they have fought in before, as a simulacrum, equipment clanking into a trough behind them. A horn. Torren, from a balcony: injury here does not count. They will face another party once that party finishes its own puzzle.

Lost City

971, the same year as Weird West. Rex Refund and Scorpion Jack went looking for the Lost City of Gold and did not find it. Nothing says where the search stopped. They are not the Heroes of Highrest, and nothing puts the two companies in the same room. From Brews to Bullion, in 980, walks the same jungle again. The dungeons, the enemies, and the locations are reused, including the Flameguard soldier still chained in the goblin pit.

What this campaign adds is the Savagebloom Expanse. Bloomridge is the Flameguard camp there, not a name in the dunes. Venomshade is the yuan-ti temple in the darkest part of that jungle, not a mark inside the Hive. The Goblin Map, the Goblin Camp, the Sunken Temple, and the River of Ssstyx are the trail. The Lost City of Gold and Death in the Jungle are blank. The cube at the end of the river has no solution written.

The next party picks the trail up under From Brews to Bullion.

Flying Boats

An expedition in 980. From Brews to Bullion is the current party, in the same year.

McMasterson captains an expedition for Emberheim into the mystery of the Plane of Air. He wants to be the one who opens relations, he met the others recently, and he trusts them about as far as their pay. Kincaid Wint carries the Auran work and, by the timeline, the Aurelic Crystal Carter left at Evershroud. Kayren, Borin Pyre, and Winston Bor are the rest of the company. Winston's change was eased. He became a full fae and left with them. Borin is the one Crazy Fog Man says was never infected. Elric Caltor watches Kincaid and is not what he seems. Their hold list is Cloudsplitter Cargo. McMasterson says they also have a ship in Emberheim. Nothing says that ship is this hold, or that they reached the Node of Air they set out for in the Frosthook mountains.

Campaign Timeline

Flying Boats keeps a second list, newest first. The two summer dates sit above the year 980 and do not name a year of their own.

Summer 23–24. The company reaches Evershroud, meets its people, and goes into the fog. They meet Mister and find The Lady of the House. They ease Winston Bor's change. He becomes a full fae, and the crowd of fae leaving the manor takes him to the Enigma. They are told of a nearby node, guarded by an Auran golem. That golem and the beast on the node are the same creature. The aarakocra crafted it from Carter Wint's old notes, and the work shows his hand. The company destroys it.

Summer 21–22. A node leads to islands isolated in the clouds. They meet couatls, find an abandoned mine, learn that Carter Wint exists, and glimpse Shakharra. They defeat an aarakocra ritual. That opens a node home for a moment. An Auran agent comes through near Azure Lagoon and is killed at once. Before he dies he names Yan-C-Bin.

980. The company sets out to investigate a new Node of Air in the Frosthook mountains.

978. The couatls send Elric Caltor into Emberheim, a couatl in disguise, to watch Kincaid and the nodes. Carter did not send him.

976. An Auran golem is sent through the node in Evershroud. The aarakocra made it by refining Carter's early Aureleonite and Aurelic Crystal work.

975. Yan-C-Bin attacks Shakharra, kills many, and possesses Carter Wint. Carter is forced back to his castle. Yan-C-Bin is physically manifest from here.

974. Carter helps Evershroud hold off the fog and plants a magic barrier in the church. He leaves an Aurelic Crystal. Kincaid Wint eventually obtains it. Through a local node he meets The Lady of the House. He meets the aarakocra, learns of Yan-C-Bin, begins to study Auran tech, and brings some home.

973. Carter Wint discovers a Node of Air in the Sea of Sand. The historical sheet's line for this year is the ports, not this node.

970. Yan-C-Bin has a minor victory against the aarakocra. Magic leaks into the physical plane. This is when the fog around Evershroud began to thicken.

97?. Kayren and his family are killed. An Auran agent replaces him and takes over his life. That agent is the player character. GM. His faceless nightmares are of Auran make. He was once something similar. He was sent through a node, barely, and told to kill that family so he could take a position of power.

From Brews to Bullion

The current party, in 980. Toshiro Haru is a human samurai fighter. Enoki Chanterelle is a moon druid and a barbarian. Marvin Giurelli Jr. is a mastermind rogue. Magnolia Oriane is a twilight cleric. No portrait, homeland, or kit is written for them. They start in Anchorfall.

It opens on a wine disaster at The Staggering Steer. The party saved Taurus Moorwind, and that is the debt his father pays.

Thaddeus Moorwind is an old human wizard. He never took a wife. He owns Moorwind Magics, the shop next door, and he adopted Taurus because he wanted to make something greater than a magic item or a magical apprentice. For the rescue he gives them his most prized object, a thing of fables he was saving until his son grew a sense of adventure. Taurus never did. The object is the Goblin Map. He says, with a smirk, that the first step will be translating it. He is also more than willing to sell the party magical objects at a discount. No figure is written for that discount.

Taurus is an adult minotaur. He owns the Steer, lives there, and brews. Enoki works under him, and the two of them are dedicated to each other. She is the one of the four who was already here. Gretchen Moorwind, his old black cat, lives in the tavern. Combat was not for him. He is happy at home, and he is terrified of the adventure Enoki is walking into. After that night the tavern and the magic shop, already neighbors, connect through the basement. The hearth, the goblets, the marble, and the cellar door are under The Staggering Steer, in Anchorfall.

The clue he hands over is the Goblin Map, the same verse Rex Refund and Scorpion Jack carried in 971 and did not finish. He says, with that smirk, that the first step is translating it. The city's own name is not chosen. Legend calls it The Lost City of Gold. An Aetherian name is still open. Decades ago, in the Era of Chaos, a wizard petrified everyone in it, soul by soul, into terracotta, and hid the crime behind a barrier that looks like more of the Savagebloom Expanse. He is still there, immortal, with that army, and he has grown stronger every year. The jungle sites come back with this party: the goblin camp, the soldier in the pit, the Sunken Temple, Venomshade, and the River of Ssstyx. Nothing says Rex and Scorpion Jack cleared any of those rooms.
