#### The Enigma

**GM, the town-level note is only a pointer.** The player sheet lists one location: Elowen's Home.

The Enigma Accident is year 683, during the Age of Terror, which the timeline opens in 666. Scraps of Disaster calls the place a magical wasteland created during the Age of Terror. Fort Ironclad watches it from the marsh and holds the Hive on the other side. The rail on the map runs to the Enigma's edge. Inside, time does not move on the things that enter. That is why Elowen hides here.

##### Elowen's Home

**GM.** Elowen Sunweaver is an elven archmage of fire and sun, now a lich, living deep in the Enigma. She founded Dusthaven. The world does not know that, does not know where she is, and does not know she is a lich. She took refuge here because the Enigma stops the flow of time on whoever is inside. She has learned the fae well enough to know they will steal a name. Inside the Enigma she goes by Newole, so the name Elowen is not available to take. The sheet spells it "the Engima" once. The Sunfire Prism's recorded voice asks the finders to come to the Enigma and ask for Newole. What the Weird West company has learned is that she created the temple that housed the prism and that she is a powerful wizard.

##### The Lady of the House

Flying Boats names her as the head of the Aetherian research at the Evershroud node. The Enigma Project poached her. The Enigma Accident hit her with enough magic to tear most people loose, and her will held. She is half-attached to her soul, and she is a sapient arch-fey. Around the year 700 she went back to the house by that node. Her rooms, and Carter Wint's visit, are under Evershroud. Nothing further is written for her.

##### Scraps of Disaster

A surreal, dangerous zone just outside the edge of that wasteland, which makes it legal to spelunk. Digs & Dives has catalogued bizarre magical artifacts here and will not explore it. The side effects of the Enigma make it too dangerous. No list of the artifacts.

##### Elowen's Journal

Entry 37 is the only entry. She has found a map on ancient vellum with no cardinal marks, only riddles.

The first: "I am where the sun kisses the earth, where shadows dance in the twilight's mirth." She reads it as the mountains, climbs to a pinnacle, and can see an endless country. The wind carries forgotten tales.

The second is still a mystery to her when she writes: "In the heart of the forest deep, where ancient oaks their secrets keep." She finds only withered husks. The leaves and the wind are gone. The forest is barren, and she blames the Sunfire Prism's heat.

The third: "By the river's gentle flow, where waters sing and memories grow." The river is long dry, taken by the same sun. She stands at a crossroads of three realms, each a witness to the prism. A fourth verse comes: "In the convergence of forest and peak, river and vale, lies the truth obscured, where elements three unveil."
