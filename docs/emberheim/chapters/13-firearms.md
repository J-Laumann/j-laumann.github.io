### Ammunition

Compact 4 gp for 20. Medium 5 gp for 20. Long 7 gp for 10. Shell 5 gp for 5. Nitro rifle rounds 20 gp for 6. Silvering one weapon, or ten pieces of ammunition, is 100 gp at Gunther's.

### Weapon traits

**Reload.** The weapon can be fired a number of times equal to its reload score. Then spend 1 attack or 1 action to reload. Reloading takes one free hand, unless the weapon is two-handed.

**Loading.** You can fire only one piece of ammunition from it when you use an action, bonus action, or reaction to fire, no matter how many attacks you have.

**Misfire.** If the attack die is equal to or lower than the misfire score, the attack misses and the weapon cannot be used until you spend an action to repair it. Tinker's tools, Dexterity, or Intelligence if the repair is out of combat. DC 8 plus the misfire score. Fail that check and the weapon is broken. Mending it out of combat costs a quarter of the firearm's price. A creature that is not proficient increases the misfire score by 1.

**Burst.** When you take the Attack action with this weapon, you can make extra attacks equal to the burst score, as part of the same action.

**Deadly.** On a critical hit, roll the weapon's damage dice one additional time.

**Ergonomic.** Being within an enemy's reach does not impose disadvantage on ranged attacks with this weapon.

**Auto-fire.** You may attack in a cone of the listed range if you have the listed amount of ammunition loaded. Each creature in the cone makes a Dexterity save against 8 plus your attack bonus with the weapon. Failure is the auto-fire score times the weapon's damage. Success is half.

**Mounted.** The weapon must be fixed to something solid. Mounting or unmounting takes an action. Unmounted, attacks have disadvantage unless the wielder's Strength equals or beats the mounted score.

**Recoil.** After each shot, ranged attack rolls with this weapon take a cumulative penalty of the listed amount if the wielder's Strength is under the listed score. The penalty clears at the start of that creature's next turn.

### Dynamite

As an action, light a stick and throw it to a point within 60 feet. Each creature within 5 feet makes a Dexterity save, DC 10 plus the thrower's Dexterity modifier. 2d6 bludgeoning, half on a success. Sticks bound together explode at once. Each extra stick adds 2d6, to a maximum of 8d6, and 5 feet of radius, to a maximum of 20 feet. A longer fuse can be cut for 1 to 6 rounds. Roll initiative for the dynamite. It explodes on its count after that many rounds.

### Accessories

**Telescopic sight, 75 gp.** Removes disadvantage at long range and imposes disadvantage at close range.

**Stock, 25 gp.** Turns a one-handed firearm two-handed and increases its range by a third.

**Sling, 25 gp.** The firearm can be holstered as a free action.

**Brawler, 35 gp.** For a firearm that is not two-handed. Choose piercing, slashing, or bludgeoning when the attachment is bought. The gun can then be used as a melee weapon, Strength for attack and damage, martial proficiency applies, 1d6 of the chosen type.

**Bladed, 35 gp.** The two-handed version of brawler. One barrel attachment at a time. 1d8 melee of the chosen type.

**Suppressor, 100 gp.** Compact or medium ammunition only. One barrel attachment at a time. Muzzle flash and sound are cut down. Creatures beyond half the weapon's close range do not hear it.

**Hanging lantern, 25 gp.** Two-handed firearms only. A lantern on the barrel: 30 feet bright, 30 feet dim, when lit.

**Choke, 25 gp.** One barrel attachment at a time. Shells only, and the firearm must be two-handed. Normal range doubles. Maximum range does not change. Disadvantage on ranged attacks with it applies inside 10 feet.

### Fanning and levering

A feat. Requires firearm proficiency. Increase Dexterity by 1. As an action, fire up to your proficiency bonus in shots, provided the ammunition is loaded. The firearm must be a revolver or a lever-action. You fan the hammer or hip-fire the lever. Every attack roll of that action is made at disadvantage.
