#### Scorpion Gulch

A three-day ride south of Dusthaven. A small town in a dusty valley of arid hills and rocky mesas. Wooden storefronts, sun-baked and sand-scoured. One dirt road, hitching posts, a few saloons with swinging doors. The general store is where locals and travelers trade news and basic supplies. The only inn is the Stinger. Cowboys, prospectors, and the occasional stranger. The economy is the nearby mines, which pull in both fortune and trouble. A harmonica, and tumbleweed on the main street. The sheriff is Wyatt Kane.

The town list names a general store, the Miner's Union, the Stinger, the church, and the sheriff. The general store sells the ordinary list in [Mundane goods](#mundane-goods). Father Mark Abrams is the priest, from the shared Church sheet. The Lost City sheet is the one that adds his first name. No separate scene was written for Wyatt, Abrams, or the gulch general store.

The Orecrusher Vault is in Miscellaneous locations.

##### The Stinger

**GM.** A swinging sign of a stylized scorpion. A faded glow spills into the street. Inside: rough beams, worn leather, a large stone fireplace, old photographs of the town, and a well-used piano that gets played when someone can play it. The waitress is Sparrow, a seasoned half-elf local.

##### Scorpion Gulch Miner's Union

**GM.** A rickety roadside building, signed Miner's Union. Discarded stone and ore sit in rusty mine carts on the porch. Clive Orecrusher waits at the desk: a bored dwarf, long gray beard, floppy wide gray hat, tiny spectacles on the tip of his nose. The sign over him says he appraises gold and gems. Heavy western accent. He will talk mountains until someone stops him. He swears he saw an ancient dwarven vault up there with his family name on it, and that "damn hell-spawn" chased him off. He figures whatever is inside would be a reclamation, not a robbery. This is while he still lives in the gulch. The Heroes of Highrest are the company that goes with him. The vault is next. Crystals and Curios comes after.


#### Steamwood

Steamwood was built to house engineers and trains, then became a workers' town. Construction brought the other blue-collar trades with it. It is still the nation's largest exporter of food and raw resources: farming, mining, logging. It sits in unexplored forest. People walk into the woods after a valuable rumor and are not heard from again.

The approach is the train: the ground goes green, the trees close in, the brakes scream, and the town is rough buildings for use rather than beauty, strung along a creek and running out of sight into the woods.

Named locations: the Axeman's Alehouse, the Steamwood Central Market, a general store, the stables, the church, and the railway station. The general store sells the ordinary list in [Mundane goods](#mundane-goods). The Graves siblings came here from Dusthaven years ago. Lucky Luke was last seen on a train in this direction. The Shattered Colossus lies in the forest to the east.

The Shattered Colossus is in Miscellaneous locations.

##### The Axeman's Alehouse

The alehouse stands at the edge of the market, grown into a huge pine whose roots are part of the town's foundations. The branches shelter a makeshift tavern. People trade adventure stories and rumors about the woods.

Grizzle Ironbark owns it. He is a long-retired worker who still keeps his axe behind the bar as a reminder of how he got the place. He looks like nonsense is unwelcome. He is kind anyway. He wants Steamwood warm and welcoming. The sheet titled Grizzle has that portrait and no separate player text.

##### The Steamwood Central Market

The player line only says the market sells adventuring gear.

**GM.** A hub in the woods. Exotic spice and fresh-cut wood. Enchanted trinkets, handcrafted leather, haggling. Wooden stalls, colored awnings, hanging lanterns. Blacksmiths, minstrels, potion vendors promising vitality and arcane wonders. At sunset the square goes to lanterns and fairy lights, children, and bards. The Axeman's Alehouse is named again at the end of this sheet, as part of the same evening.
