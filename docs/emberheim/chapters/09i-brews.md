### From Brews to Bullion

The current party, in 980. Toshiro Haru is a human samurai fighter. Enoki Chanterelle is a moon druid and a barbarian. Marvin Giurelli Jr. is a mastermind rogue. Magnolia Oriane is a twilight cleric. No portrait, homeland, or kit is written for them. They start in Anchorfall.

It opens on a wine disaster at The Staggering Steer. The party saved Taurus Moorwind, and that is the debt his father pays.

Thaddeus Moorwind is an old human wizard. He never took a wife. He owns Moorwind Magics, the shop next door, and he adopted Taurus because he wanted to make something greater than a magic item or a magical apprentice. For the rescue he gives them his most prized object, a thing of fables he was saving until his son grew a sense of adventure. Taurus never did. The object is the Goblin Map. He says, with a smirk, that the first step will be translating it. He is also more than willing to sell the party magical objects at a discount. No figure is written for that discount.

Taurus is an adult minotaur. He owns the Steer, lives there, and brews. Enoki works under him, and the two of them are dedicated to each other. She is the one of the four who was already here. Gretchen Moorwind, his old black cat, lives in the tavern. Combat was not for him. He is happy at home, and he is terrified of the adventure Enoki is walking into. After that night the tavern and the magic shop, already neighbors, connect through the basement. The hearth, the goblets, the marble, and the cellar door are under The Staggering Steer, in Anchorfall.

The clue he hands over is the Goblin Map, the same verse Rex Refund and Scorpion Jack carried in 971 and did not finish. He says, with that smirk, that the first step is translating it. The city's own name is not chosen. Legend calls it The Lost City of Gold. An Aetherian name is still open. Decades ago, in the Era of Chaos, a wizard petrified everyone in it, soul by soul, into terracotta, and hid the crime behind a barrier that looks like more of the Savagebloom Expanse. He is still there, immortal, with that army, and he has grown stronger every year. The jungle sites come back with this party: the goblin camp, the soldier in the pit, the Sunken Temple, Venomshade, and the River of Ssstyx. Nothing says Rex and Scorpion Jack cleared any of those rooms.
