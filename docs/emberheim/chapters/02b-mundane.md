#### Mundane

A smaller town on the central continent, north of Ironspire, in wooded hills that are just beginning to turn cold. A small sea lies between it and the Enigma. It is where people go to hone traditional magic, and the railway north ends beyond it at Glacier's Edge, about 125 miles, a day at 12 hours 30 minutes, 10 gp.

##### The College of Mundane

The school of the town. Anybody in the world may come looking for knowledge of magics. No masters, tuition, or library list are written.
