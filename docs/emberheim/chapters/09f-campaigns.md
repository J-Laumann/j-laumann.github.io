## Campaigns

Weird West's party is the Heroes of Highrest. Lost City is the same year, a party of two who did not find the Lost City of Gold. Flying Boats is an expedition in 980. From Brews to Bullion is the current party, also in 980: Toshiro Haru, Enoki Chanterelle, Marvin Giurelli Jr., and Magnolia Oriane. It opens on the wine disaster at The Staggering Steer, and Thaddeus Moorwind pays the rescuers with a clue to the city those two never found.
