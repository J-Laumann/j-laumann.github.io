Ironspire and the Ember Plains. The Council sits in Ironspire. The crew on the capital's own work is the Rust Rats.

### The Rust Rats

The crew Meryn Bronzethumb hires to steal Dr. Verid Threx's plans for the Arcstrike Cannon. Jessa "Redwren" Skorr leads them, a human rogue with mechanical limbs. They are paid to be efficient and unknown. They eat Threx's vault door with a brew of rust-monster antennae and deliver the plans. One sentence of that sheet also calls them the Rustcloaks. The name is the Rust Rats. No other sheet mentions them.
