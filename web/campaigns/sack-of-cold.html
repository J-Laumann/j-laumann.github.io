Sack of Cold

GM. The sack leads to the Ninth Circle. The party was hired to find Saint Nicholas. They succeeded, brought back his corpse and others, and killed Velkhana, the current Guardian of Souls. Pay was 15 platinum each. They were offered a sale of their Velkhana crystal, one of two. Will settled on 250 gp a week of research. The crystal would be returned with the findings.

Nicholas revives and recovers. A week later he finds the party, thanks them, says he knew they could do it, and introduces himself to anyone new. He answers questions, presents what he actually found, and tells them to apply as heroes. Hiring "randos he knew" cost him a miserable amount of searching and paperwork.

The circle itself, Krampus's gate, and the Pine ranch storm are under The Ninth Circle and Dusthaven.
